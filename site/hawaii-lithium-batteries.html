HAWAII SHIPPING

Hawaii Shipping Available on Eligible Lithium Batteries

Shipping costs and methods may differ from mainland orders. Elevation verifies Hawaii eligibility product by product before advertising an approved island rate.

For products that have not yet been verified, we do not guess the charge. Those products remain marked Hawaii Shipping Quote Required until a supplier or freight option is confirmed.

Phase 1 shipping safeguards
  • No free Hawaii shipping claims.
  • No assumption that every lithium battery can ship to Hawaii.
  • No Hawaii direct-checkout rate until the product rule is verified.
  • Mainland supplier shipping stays separate from Hawaii shipping.
