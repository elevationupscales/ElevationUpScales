HOW HAWAII ORDERS WORK

One storefront model, one product-aware request.

1 · Choose the exact SOK product

Product identity stays attached to the request. Elevation does not create a second Hawaii-only inventory record.

2 · Open Purchase Options

Hawaii mode preselects HI. Add quantity, ZIP, island and one contact method.

3 · Elevation confirms the route

Supply, freight and the next executable purchase step are confirmed before the order advances.
