SHIPPING EDUCATION

Why large lithium batteries cost more to ship to Hawaii

For Elevation’s Hawaii program, the shipping price can include more than ordinary parcel postage. The exact battery and model have to be matched to the available transportation information, then the shipment may involve documentation review, packaging and terminal-protection checks, mainland origin movement, consolidation and handling, specialized freight-provider handling, ocean movement, terminal or accessorial charges, and destination delivery.

Those steps are presented here as Elevation’s operating process and cost model. Applicable transportation requirements depend on the exact battery, configuration, mode of transport, and carrier or freight provider. Elevation does not act as a regulator or certify a product for transportation.

Reference: U.S. DOT Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration (PHMSA), Lithium Battery Guide and Lithium Battery Test Summary resources; 49 CFR §173.185. PHMSA lithium battery resources · 49 CFR §173.185
