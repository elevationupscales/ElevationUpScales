IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION

Button-cell ingestion hazard.
This product contains a button cell or coin battery. Keep new and used batteries out of reach of children. Seek immediate medical attention if a battery is suspected to have been swallowed or inserted into any part of the body.

Mounting note from the supplied launch sheet: the ORS-153 M5 screw should be installed with non-permanent medium/blue threadlocker and torqued to 43 in-lbs. Refer to the official user manual and safety material before installation or use.
