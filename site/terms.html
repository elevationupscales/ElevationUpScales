Business identity

Elevation UpScales, Inc. is a Colorado corporation and holds a Colorado sales-tax license. The sales-tax license is separate from trade-specific, contractor, carrier, manufacturer or transportation qualifications.

Licensed Lithium Battery Retailer

Licensed Lithium Battery Retailer. Elevation UpScales, Inc. is a Colorado corporation and licensed retail business offering lithium batteries and related RV, solar, and off-grid power products through its retail operations. The term “Licensed Lithium Battery Retailer” describes Elevation UpScales, Inc.’s licensed retail status and the product category it offers for sale. Manufacturer authorization, carrier approval, hazardous-materials qualifications, and transportation certifications are separate statuses and are represented only when specifically applicable.

Product, inventory and pricing

Published product availability, supplier inventory, prices and shipping options can change. Elevation uses its current Catalog and supplier information when presenting products and rechecks applicable order values during checkout or freight review. Internal supplier cost is not a customer-facing price.

Lithium shipping

Lower-48 shipping for actual lithium batteries is currently $27.99 per actual battery where standard lithium checkout is supported. Alaska service and pricing remain quote required where exact routing has not been verified.

Hawaii lithium batteries use consolidated freight. The current customer freight price is $99 per actual battery, separate from merchandise. Orders of fewer than three compatible batteries may wait while Elevation combines compatible Hawaii orders. Three compatible batteries is the current preferred shipment target, not a guaranteed departure trigger.

The standard Hawaii freight price covers Honolulu warehouse or freight-terminal pickup. Delivery from the pickup location to a home, business, jobsite or other address is not included and may be arranged separately for an additional quoted charge where available. Shipment, pickup and any separately arranged final-mile delivery timing is estimated and not guaranteed; timing can change because of consolidation, supplier inventory, exact battery documentation, packaging, actual packed gross weight, carrier scheduling, mainland receiving, ocean freight and Hawaii terminal availability.

Transportation roles

Elevation may coordinate exact product/SKU verification, supplier availability, SDS/MSDS and UN38.3 documentation where required, packed dimensions and gross weight, freight documentation, mainland receiving, consolidation, freight booking, Hawaii movement, terminal coordination and customer pickup notification. Elevation does not represent itself as a regulator, testing laboratory, battery manufacturer or freight carrier unless a separate status is specifically stated.

Extraordinary route changes

If an extraordinary carrier or route change creates a materially different customer requirement before shipment, Elevation will disclose that situation before collecting an additional charge or tendering the shipment. Additional freight charges are not silently added.
