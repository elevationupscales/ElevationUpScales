Privacy Notice

How we use lead information.

Elevation UpScales, Inc. collects only the information needed to respond to project requests, save solar-system builds, review compatibility, and follow up with customers.

Legal business identity

Elevation UpScales, Inc. is a Colorado corporation and holds a Colorado sales-tax license. This sales-tax license is separate from any trade-specific or contractor licensing requirements.

Information we collect

Depending on the form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, city and state, RV or marketplace-item information, project details, selected solar components, preferred contact method, and listing photos.

Marketplace submissions

Marketplace submissions are stored privately while they wait for administrator review. If a listing is approved, the item details, city and state, condition disclosure, asking price, and submitted photos may be published. Seller email addresses are kept out of the public listing. A seller phone number may be displayed only after a buyer selects the Show Contact Info control.

How we use it

We use this information to save requests, review marketplace posts, contact sellers or customers, publish approved listings, review system compatibility, prepare recommendations or quotes, and maintain business records. A consent checkbox is not used to subscribe someone to unrelated marketing.

Anonymous site and Marketplace analytics

We may measure page visits and Marketplace actions such as category selection, listing opens, likes, shares, seller-contact reveals, call/text button clicks, and listing-submission steps so we can understand whether the site is working and improve it. Elevation UpScales, Inc. does not store a buyer name, email address, phone number, raw IP address, or search text in this first-party Marketplace analytics record. A temporary anonymous browser-session identifier is used to distinguish activity during a visit. Security and health-monitoring requests are kept separate from these customer-event records.

Local browser storage

The marketplace may store your Liked listing choices and unfinished seller-form drafts locally in your browser or device so those preferences can remain available when you return; unfinished seller drafts expire after seven days. The Solar Builder may store non-contact equipment selections locally, while contact details, free-text solar notes, and lead-result state are limited to the current browser tab session and are cleared when that session ends. This browser data is not a customer account and can be removed by clearing site data in your browser.

Service providers and storage

Submissions may be processed through Cloudflare services. Marketplace records may be stored in a private Cloudflare D1 database, and submitted listing photos may be stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket. Email is used to notify Elevation UpScales, Inc. that a submission is waiting for review.

Retention and requests

Information is retained only as reasonably needed for moderation, follow-up, active listings, project records, safety, and business administration. To request access, correction, unpublishing, or deletion, email casey@elevationupscales.com or call 208-813-4998.

Preliminary system builder

The solar generator is a planning tool. Submitted equipment selections are not a final electrical design, compatibility approval, or installed-price commitment.
