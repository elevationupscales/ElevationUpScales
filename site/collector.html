Why the Cards Are Collectible

Business cards worth keeping

Most business cards are looked at once and thrown away. The Elevation UpScales Collector Series was created to be different.

Each edition has its own artwork, identity, story, and place in the complete four-card collection. Every card also includes real company contact information and access to a $50 new-client coupon.

The first release introduces the Collector Series concept. Future releases may use upgraded materials, heavier card stock, metallic finishes, foil details, holographic effects, numbered editions, limited print quantities, seasonal artwork, and rare variants.

The goal is to create cards that customers want to keep, display, trade, and collect.

Promotional Terms

Official promotion details

View the promotion rules

A qualifying complete set consists of the four original physical Collector Series cards:

  • Signature Edition
  • Emergency Response Edition
  • Bull Edition
  • Polar Bear Edition

Screenshots, photocopies, scans, reproductions, altered cards, and counterfeit cards do not qualify unless Elevation UpScales provides written approval.

A verified qualifying set may be redeemed for one $1,000 Golden Ticket retail credit.

The credit:

  • Has no cash value
  • Cannot be exchanged for cash
  • Cannot create a cash refund
  • May only be redeemed through Elevation UpScales
  • May only be used toward qualifying purchases, products, materials, service packages, or approved projects
  • Is subject to availability, scheduling, written estimates, and applicable service agreements
  • May be subject to additional written campaign terms

Elevation UpScales may reject cards that are duplicated, altered, reproduced, counterfeit, damaged beyond verification, or otherwise not verifiable as original campaign cards.

Elevation UpScales may record redeemed sets and mark, retain, or invalidate cards after redemption to prevent duplicate claims.
