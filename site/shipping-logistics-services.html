THE ROUTE

Keep the customer path simple.

1 · Choose the exact product

Start with the SOK catalog, lithium shop or current Elevation product listing.

2 · Add quantity and destination

The purchase-options path carries the SKU forward so the logistics review stays tied to the product.

3 · Confirm the executable route

Elevation checks the applicable supply and shipping path without exposing private carrier or supplier operations.

4 · Advance the purchase

The order proceeds only through the purchase or assisted-fulfillment path that is actually supported.

ALASKA

Destination-specific shipping review

Alaska is exposed as a logistics-review destination, not as a blanket shipping promise. Exact eligibility can vary by SKU, supplier source and shipping method.

  • Choose the exact product before requesting a route.
  • Enter AK and the destination ZIP in SOK Purchase Options.
  • Elevation confirms whether the current product can use an executable shipping path.
  • Unsupported routes remain review-only and do not become implied availability.
