## MAP condition

The authorization is paired with a MAP requirement. SOK stated that Elevation should follow the MAP listed on the applicable price sheet / SOK retail guidance when uploading SOK products.

Do not use this record to justify:

- pricing below applicable SOK MAP,
- representing Elevation as the manufacturer,
- representing Elevation as SOK Energy itself,
- claiming that SOK is the freight carrier,
- copying unrelated SOK creative assets where permission has not been established,
- or claiming broader trademark rights than SOK actually granted.
