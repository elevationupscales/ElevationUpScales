# Elevation UpScales — Marketplace Working-Capital Recovery Standard

**Date:** 2026-09-11 MDT  
**Owner:** Casey Young  
**Issued by:** PM4 / Hybrid Management  
**Applies to:** eBay + TikTok Shop marketplace execution lanes  
**Parent controls:** `MASTER_SOP_V1_0.md`; `PROFITABILITY_RECOVERY_WORKFLOW_V1_0.md`; `PROFITABILITY_RECOVERY_CASH_BASELINE_2026-09-11.md`  
**State:** ACTIVE / CASH-FLOW RECOVERY

## Owner direction

Elevation must restore cash flow so marketplace sales can fund their own next orders instead of requiring repeated owner cash injections.

Current owner startup-cash estimate: approximately **$700**.

Current operating baseline:

- PayPal liquid cash: **$1.00**;
- eBay available: **$0.00**;
- eBay held: **$242.79**;
- TikTok held: **$222.25**;
- total verified held marketplace receivables: **$465.04**.

The marketplace problem is therefore a **cash-conversion-cycle problem**, not simply a sales-volume problem.

## Controlling marketplace objective

**SELL PROFITABLY → FUND THE ORDER → SHIP FAST → RELEASE PAYOUT → RECYCLE CASH → REPEAT.**

Do not optimize either marketplace for GMV alone.

## Required SKU gate

Before a marketplace SKU is intentionally promoted, newly activated, materially expanded or restored from a hold, verify:

1. exact SKU/source identity;
2. marketplace/channel authorization;
3. current inventory/sellability;
4. current landed supplier cost;
5. MAP/price compliance where applicable;
6. platform fees;
7. affiliate/ad/discount leakage where applicable;
8. expected contribution;
9. company cash required before payout release;
10. supplier processing/tracking speed;
11. known return/refund risk;
12. payout/hold risk.

The product must be both **profitable** and **fundable**.

## Free working capital

`FREE WORKING CAPITAL = LIQUID CASH - OPEN-ORDER SUPPLIER COMMITMENTS - REFUND/CHARGEBACK/CUSTOMER-PROTECTION RESERVE`

Held marketplace proceeds do not count until released.

## Recovery exposure rule

Until Elevation has at least **$500 free working capital**:

- avoid intentional marketplace expansion when one normal order requires supplier prepayment above **25% of free working capital**;
- keep total open marketplace supplier-prepayment exposure at or below **50% of free working capital**, absent Casey approval;
- this controls promotion/activation/expansion, not fulfillment of a customer obligation already created;
- do not generically shut off functioning checkout because of the recovery rule; instead control which exact SKUs are promoted and how much concurrent exposure is allowed.

At the current `$1.00` liquid baseline, the immediate marketplace task is **cash release + order cleanup + profitable-core preservation**, not high-COGS volume expansion.

## eBay lane

The existing eBay profitability recovery controls remain in force:

**CUSTOMER FIRST → CASH RELEASE → STOP NEW LOSSES → SMALL VERIFIED CORE → POSITIVE CONTRIBUTION → CLEAN FULFILLMENT → SCALE.**

Additional cash-flow requirement:

- rank retained/rebuilt listings by realized or expected profit per dollar of supplier cash that must be pre-funded;
- prefer lower-COGS, faster-turn products when margins are otherwise acceptable;
- do not use promoted listings by default during recovery;
- held funds are not available cash;
- preserve useful sales/watchers history only when the rebuilt SKU can satisfy both contribution and float requirements.

## TikTok Shop lane

TikTok Shop must be managed as a profitable marketplace lane, not as a GMV scoreboard.

For each retained/expanded SKU account for:

- supplier landed cost;
- TikTok fees;
- seller-funded discounts;
- creator/affiliate commission;
- samples where seller-funded;
- shipping or fulfillment costs borne by Elevation;
- refund/return exposure;
- cash pre-funded before settlement release.

During cash recovery:

- prioritize products that can generate positive contribution with low supplier cash pre-funding;
- avoid increasing creator commission or free-sample spend solely to chase traffic when exact economics do not support it;
- refundable samples remain preferable to free samples where appropriate;
- settlement/account-health recovery is a cash-flow task because held funds cannot support the next fulfillment cycle;
- high-COGS products may remain listed when already authorized, but do not intentionally drive volume beyond the working-capital float.

## Direct-site relationship

The direct Elevation PayPal lane remains the preferred recovery engine because customer cash is captured before supplier purchase under the normal order-first path.

Marketplace managers should not compete with or displace the P0 direct-site first-profit lane while the company remains below the `$500` free-working-capital target.

## Released-fund routing

When marketplace funds actually release:

1. cover any exact remaining obligation tied to the originating order;
2. reserve for known refunds/chargebacks/customer protection;
3. retain the remaining cash inside the company until at least `$500` free working capital exists;
4. continue building toward `$1,000` free working capital;
5. after the reserve is stable, apply realized profit to the startup-deficit recovery ledger under the cash baseline.

## Cash-return efficiency

For ranking marketplace products:

`CASH RETURN EFFICIENCY = REALIZED OR EXPECTED CONTRIBUTION / MAXIMUM COMPANY CASH PRE-FUNDED BEFORE CUSTOMER PROCEEDS BECOME AVAILABLE`

Higher is better, provided fulfillment reliability and customer risk remain acceptable.

A lower-revenue SKU can be superior to a higher-revenue SKU when it returns more contribution per dollar of scarce cash and turns that cash faster.

## Required reporting from each marketplace lane

Each marketplace Worktree should surface, for its core revenue SKUs:

- current selling price;
- landed cost;
- expected contribution;
- actual contribution after sale;
- cash required before payout;
- payout/hold state;
- fulfillment time;
- refund/return outcome;
- keep / reprice / resource / hold / end decision.

Do not duplicate these details into the Master Workboard SKU by SKU. Escalate only material cash-flow exceptions and aggregate state.

## Close condition

This temporary recovery standard can be relaxed only after:

- at least **$500 free working capital** is verified;
- open customer obligations are funded;
- eBay/TikTok core SKUs show positive realized contribution;
- payout/hold behavior is understood well enough to support repeat orders without owner cash injections.

Normal profitability controls remain after the temporary exposure caps are relaxed.

## Control phrase

**GMV DOES NOT FUND THE NEXT ORDER — LIQUID CASH DOES. SELL WHAT MAKES MONEY, FAVOR WHAT TURNS CASH FAST, RELEASE THE HOLDS, AND RECYCLE THE FLOAT.**
