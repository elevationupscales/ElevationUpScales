## Management interpretation

Elevation is currently operating as a **near-zero-liquid-cash startup with a negative marketplace cash-conversion cycle**.

The company has generated sales, but eBay/TikTok settlement holds can trap customer proceeds after Elevation has already had to fund supplier fulfillment. That can make additional sales consume cash faster than the platforms release it.

Therefore the immediate problem is not simply revenue generation. It is:

**PROFITABLE SALES + ENOUGH LIQUID FLOAT TO FUND THE NEXT ORDER BEFORE THE LAST PAYOUT RELEASES.**

Held funds must not be spent, promised or counted as working capital before release.

## Free working capital definition

For management purposes:

`FREE WORKING CAPITAL = LIQUID CASH - SUPPLIER CASH COMMITTED TO OPEN ORDERS - REFUND / CHARGEBACK / CUSTOMER-PROTECTION RESERVE`

Only **free working capital** may be used to decide how aggressively eBay/TikTok can scale.

A platform balance that is held, pending, restricted or already economically committed to an open customer obligation is **not free working capital**.

## Cash hierarchy

Until normal working capital is restored:

1. **Protect existing customer obligations.**
2. **Release already-earned eBay/TikTok cash.**
3. **Keep direct Elevation PayPal sales first because customer cash can be captured before supplier purchase.**
4. **Keep eBay/TikTok open as profitable sales channels, but scale them only to the level the real cash float can support.**
5. **Do not use expected/held funds to justify purchases.**
6. **Do not buy speculative inventory by default.**
7. **No paid advertising until a product proves conversion and positive realized contribution.**
8. **Record actual contribution and cash pre-funding requirement after every sale.**

## Marketplace cash-cycle rule

Marketplace growth must be governed by both **profitability** and **cash requirement**.

Every eBay/TikTok product intended for active promotion or expansion must pass:

**EXACT SKU → CURRENT SOURCE → LANDED COST → PLATFORM FEES → DISCOUNTS/COMMISSIONS → EXPECTED CONTRIBUTION → CASH REQUIRED BEFORE PAYOUT → FULFILLMENT SPEED → PAYOUT/HOLD RISK.**

A profitable product can still be a bad recovery product when it requires too much cash to fund before the marketplace releases proceeds.

### Exposure limits during recovery

Until Elevation has at least `$500` in verified free working capital:

- do not intentionally expand/promote a marketplace SKU when one ordinary order would require supplier prepayment greater than **25% of current free working capital**;
- total open marketplace supplier-prepayment exposure should remain at or below **50% of current free working capital** unless Casey explicitly clears the exception;
- these limits govern promotion, activation and expansion—not abandonment of an already-created customer obligation;
- do **not** generically disable working checkout merely because cash is tight; control which SKUs are promoted/activated and how much concurrent exposure is allowed.

With only `$1.00` currently liquid, the immediate marketplace priority is therefore **release existing cash + fulfill/protect existing obligations + preserve only profitable core offers**, not aggressive new high-COGS order acquisition.

## Recovery stages

### Stage 0 — Survival / liquidity

**Current state. Goal:** prevent the next sale from causing an unfundable obligation.

- `$1.00` liquid baseline;
- no discretionary spend;
- no speculative inventory;
- no paid ads;
- no avoidable software subscriptions;
- no unsupported discounts;
- no marketplace expansion that depends on nonexistent cash float;
- existing customer obligations remain protected.

### Stage 1 — Unlock earned cash

**Goal:** convert the verified `$465.04` held marketplace receivables into liquid cash without creating additional losses.

- eBay specialist owns eBay hold/order recovery;
- TikTok lane owns TikTok settlement/account-health recovery;
- held balances stay separate from available cash;
- first use of released funds is to cover any exact originating-order obligation still outstanding;
- remaining released cash stays inside the business as working capital.

### Stage 2 — Marketplace operating float

**Goal:** reach **$500 free working capital after open-order obligations are reserved**.

Until this is reached, prioritize:

1. direct-site PayPal sales with positive verified contribution;
2. low-cash-requirement eBay/TikTok winners;
3. fast supplier fulfillment and tracking;
4. products with low refund/return risk;
5. products generating the most realized contribution per dollar of supplier cash pre-funded.

### Stage 3 — Working-capital floor

**Goal:** reach **$1,000 free unrestricted business cash**.

At this stage Elevation can support a wider set of eBay/TikTok orders without one or two holds starving the next fulfillment cycle.

Expansion remains contingent on positive actual contribution and clean fulfillment.

### Stage 4 — Startup-capital recovery / break-even

After at least `$500` free working capital is protected, track the owner's approximately `$700` startup-capital estimate against **realized cumulative contribution**, not gross sales.

The current provisional gross gap is approximately `$233.96`, but the actual remaining break-even amount must be adjusted for unresolved fulfillment costs, refunds, fees and any startup expenses not yet captured.

Do not call Elevation fully **out of the red** until the verified startup-deficit ledger reaches `$0` while the working-capital reserve remains intact.

### Stage 5 — Scale

Scale only products/channels that show:

**DEMAND + VERIFIED SOURCE + POSITIVE REALIZED CONTRIBUTION + CLEAN FULFILLMENT + ACCEPTABLE CASH CYCLE.**

## SKU ranking during liquidity recovery

When two products are both profitable, prioritize the one that returns more profit per dollar of cash tied up.

Useful management metric:

`CASH RETURN EFFICIENCY = EXPECTED OR REALIZED CONTRIBUTION / MAXIMUM COMPANY CASH PRE-FUNDED BEFORE CUSTOMER PROCEEDS BECOME AVAILABLE`

Prefer products with:

- lower landed supplier cost;
- faster tracking and delivery;
- lower return risk;
- stronger margin after all channel costs;
- lower affiliate/ad leakage;
- faster payout release;
- reliable stock.
