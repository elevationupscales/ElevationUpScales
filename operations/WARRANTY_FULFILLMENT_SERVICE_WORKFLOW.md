## SERVICE NAME

**Warranty Fulfillment**

This is an Elevation UpScales service line being built for manufacturers, distributors, and approved retail partners that need regional execution of warranty obligations in markets where they do not maintain complete local fulfillment, storage, or reverse-logistics infrastructure.

Initial proving market: Hawaii lithium battery / energy-storage products.
Initial anchor supplier relationship: SOK Energy.

SOK is the founding proof program, not an exclusive platform unless Casey separately authorizes exclusivity.

## CORE POSITIONING

Elevation does not replace the manufacturer's warranty authority.

The manufacturer determines warranty coverage and authorization unless a written agreement later delegates additional authority.

Elevation executes the regional fulfillment layer after or alongside supplier authorization.

Core value proposition:

**MANUFACTURER WARRANTY DECISION → ELEVATION REGIONAL EXECUTION → CUSTOMER RESOLUTION → SUPPLIER REPLENISHMENT / FINANCIAL RECONCILIATION**

## STANDARD WORKFLOW

**WARRANTY INTAKE**
→ customer / purchaser / referring dealer identified
→ order, SKU, serial, purchase date, symptoms and evidence captured

**VERIFY / TRIAGE**
→ confirm eligibility and evidence
→ separate support/install issue from possible product defect
→ route safety concerns separately

**MANUFACTURER AUTHORIZATION**
→ supplier approves repair, component replacement, full replacement, return or disposition

**INVENTORY / SOURCE DECISION**
→ local Elevation-controlled stock if authorized and available
→ supplier/mainland allocation if local stock unavailable
→ never claim inventory reserved until physically controlled or supplier allocation is confirmed

**FULFILLMENT**
→ reserve exact serial
→ warehouse release / will-call / local delivery / approved onward route
→ proof of release retained

**FAILED-UNIT CONTROL**
→ returned/failed units never re-enter saleable inventory
→ quarantine, hold, return, recycle, destroy or consolidate only under verified supplier / warehouse / carrier instructions

**REPLENISHMENT / FINANCIAL RECONCILIATION**
→ replacement inventory replenishment, account credit, reimbursement or other supplier remedy recorded
→ freight, warehouse, delivery, handling and Elevation service cost allocated to the case

**CUSTOMER CLOSEOUT**
→ customer receives documented resolution
→ case status closed only after operational and financial reconciliation is complete or an approved exception is recorded

**PROGRAM DATA**
→ claim reason, SKU, cost, resolution time, inventory use, supplier support and customer outcome added to the program dataset
→ stock levels are later driven by real claim rate, sales velocity and replenishment lead time

## ECONOMIC CONTROL

Product replenishment alone is not a complete Warranty Fulfillment compensation model.

A supplier replacing the physical unit restores inventory value, but Elevation may still incur:

- inbound / inter-island / mainland-to-Hawaii freight allocation;
- dangerous-goods / hazmat coordination;
- receiving and storage;
- serialized inventory administration;
- pick, release and customer handoff;
- local delivery;
- case administration and customer communication;
- failed-unit quarantine / reverse logistics;
- inventory carrying cost and working-capital exposure.

Permanent supplier programs must therefore reconcile both **product** and **regional execution cost**.

Possible mature commercial components include:

1. supplier replenishment / product credit;
2. freight or logistics credit / reimbursement;
3. storage contribution where inventory is dedicated to supplier warranty readiness;
4. per-case Warranty Fulfillment fee;
5. monthly regional readiness / platform fee;
6. warehouse and local-delivery pass-through or contracted margin;
7. inventory-financing / consignment structure where commercially justified;
8. referral / distribution economics created by the regional program.

No fee structure is considered approved until Casey authorizes it.

## SOK FOUNDING PROGRAM — CURRENT COMMERCIAL POSITION

SOK has provided a valuable starting structure by allowing Elevation-held inventory to be used for manufacturer-authorized replacements and replenishing used replacement inventory into later Elevation orders.

That offer should be preserved and developed, but not treated by itself as a complete long-term economic arrangement.

The project objective is to negotiate a stronger, balanced pilot structure in which:

**Elevation contributes**
- disciplined local inventory;
- customer-facing coordination;
- evidence / serial tracking;
- warehouse and release execution;
- program reporting;
- market development.

**SOK contributes**
- warranty authorization and technical support;
- product / parts replenishment;
- defined reasonable warranty-freight support;
- escalation support;
- warranty-history / reserve guidance;
- Hawaii customer / dealer referrals where appropriate.

A permanent arrangement should be reviewed after real operating data exists rather than by assuming Elevation will indefinitely absorb regional logistics overhead.

## WAREHOUSE CONTROL

Hawaii storage is infrastructure for Elevation's broader business, not a free dedicated warehouse benefit for any supplier.

If supplier warranty inventory uses capacity Elevation was already establishing for normal Hawaii commerce, that use must still be measured and economically reconciled.

The preferred first-stage model is shared new, saleable inventory that remains available for normal customer sale until specifically reserved against an authorized warranty claim, unless the supplier requires separate warranty-only inventory.

Warehouse capacity may support multiple supplier programs, but inventory, cases, costs and supplier data must remain segregated by vendor.

**ONE PLATFORM → MULTIPLE PRIVATE WARRANTY PROGRAMS**

## MULTI-VENDOR PLATFORM DIRECTION

Once the SOK workflow is proven, Warranty Fulfillment may be offered to additional manufacturers, distributors or retailers that need Hawaii regional execution.

Each supplier must have its own program profile covering:

- warranty authority;
- customer eligibility;
- evidence / diagnostic requirements;
- replacement and parts rules;
- inventory ownership;
- stock replenishment;
- freight responsibility;
- failed-unit disposition;
- warehouse requirements;
- service compensation;
- escalation contacts;
- SLA only when verified and approved.

Shared infrastructure does not mean shared supplier economics or data.

## STRATEGIC RULE

The SOK relationship should be treated as the founding proof relationship and developed with give-and-take.

Elevation should be willing to carry a limited, measured amount of pilot effort to prove capability and build trust, but it must measure every real cost so successful execution can later support a fair sustainable commercial structure.

**PROVE VALUE → MEASURE COST → BUILD TRUST → NEGOTIATE FROM RESULTS → SCALE WHAT WORKS**
