## SERVICE NAME

**Warranty Fulfillment**

Warranty Fulfillment is the Elevation UpScales service being built to support manufacturers, distributors, and approved partners that need regional execution of an authorized warranty resolution.

The service name is locked.

## CORE WORKFLOW

**WARRANTY INTAKE**
→ identify customer, order, product/SKU, serial number, purchase information, issue and available evidence

**VERIFY / TRIAGE**
→ verify the case information and route the matter to the applicable manufacturer/supplier warranty authority

**AUTHORIZATION**
→ manufacturer/supplier determines the approved warranty resolution unless a future written agreement delegates additional authority

**FULFILLMENT**
→ execute the authorized repair-part, replacement-product, return, local release, delivery, or other approved fulfillment path

**FAILED-UNIT CONTROL**
→ keep failed/returned product separate from saleable inventory and follow the verified supplier, warehouse, carrier, safety, and disposition requirements

**REPLENISHMENT / RECONCILIATION**
→ record replacement inventory, credit, reimbursement, replenishment, freight, handling, and other case reconciliation required by the applicable vendor program

**CUSTOMER CLOSEOUT**
→ confirm the authorized resolution was completed and close the case with supporting records

## CONTROL

Each manufacturer/vendor may have different warranty rules, authority, evidence requirements, inventory arrangements, freight responsibility, failed-unit procedures, and commercial terms.

Those supplier-specific terms belong in that supplier's project or program record and are **not** defined by this shared service workflow.
