## Warm-lead principle

Existing warm creators outrank generic cold-volume outreach when they have already:

- accepted an invitation;
- added Elevation products to showcase;
- requested/received a sample;
- posted content;
- generated clicks or orders;
- demonstrated strong audience fit.

Do not abandon warm relationships simply because a new invite batch begins.

## Profitability gate

No affiliate product may be scaled without current approved economics from Apparel Vendor Operations.

For every campaign track:

**SALE PRICE → PROVIDER COST → TIKTOK/CHANNEL FEES → AFFILIATE COMMISSION → PROMO/DISCOUNT → SAMPLE COST/ALLOCATION → EXPECTED NET CONTRIBUTION → REALIZED CONTRIBUTION.**

GMV is not success if the contribution is negative or payout/fulfillment risk worsens.
