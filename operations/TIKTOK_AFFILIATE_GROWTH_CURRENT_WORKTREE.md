**SALE PRICE → PROVIDER/SOURCE COST → SHIPPING SUBSIDY → TIKTOK/CHANNEL FEES → AFFILIATE COMMISSION → PROMO/DISCOUNT → AUTHORIZED SAMPLE COST/ALLOCATION IF ANY → EXPECTED NET CONTRIBUTION → REALIZED CONTRIBUTION.**
