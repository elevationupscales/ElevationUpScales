# RENOGY MASTER CATALOG — UPPER MANAGEMENT

Owner: Casey Young  
Company: Elevation UpScales, Inc.  
Date: 2026-09-17  
Scope: Lower 48 Renogy dealer catalog and activation controls

## SECTION 1 — MASTER PRODUCT CATALOG

Source basis: current Renogy dealer item workbook supplied to Elevation UpScales. The working catalog contains 204 dealer rows with exact item number, description, dealer price, MSRP, status and UPC where supplied.

Management rule: exact dealer SKU is the identity key. Shopify or storefront records must reconcile to the dealer SKU before activation. Existing records are corrected in place rather than duplicated.

Pricing rule: dealer Price is the current cost reference from the supplied workbook; MSRP is the management retail reference. Public Renogy pricing must be checked again before activation when a current public price differs from the workbook.

Catalog use: prioritize conversion products, solar panels/controllers, battery monitoring, inverters, DC-DC charging, complete solar systems and proven accessories. Lithium products remain subject to lithium-specific shipping and fulfillment controls.

## SECTION 2 — WARRANTY, BACKORDER & ACTIVATION CONTROL

Warranty: preserve exact product/component warranty terms. Do not apply a generic Renogy warranty to every SKU. Warranty claims must be tied to the exact product or component warranty source.

Backorder: a public Renogy backorder indication is not equivalent to dealer orderability. Before a paid listing is activated, verify the exact SKU through the current Partner Portal/order path. Do not represent a backordered or unverified SKU as physically in stock.

Activation gate: exact SKU -> price/profit check -> current public-price check where applicable -> warranty source -> Partner Portal orderability -> approved media -> shipping route -> Shopify publication -> live purchase smoke test.

Current merchandising direction: build a conversion ladder from low-friction entry products and monitoring accessories through panels/controllers and into complete high-dollar systems. Do not force ambiguous legacy variants into an activation wave.

This management file accompanies the complete catalog workbook and is intended as the control summary, not a replacement for row-level SKU data.
