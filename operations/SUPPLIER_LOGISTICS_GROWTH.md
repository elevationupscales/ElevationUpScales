# Elevation UpScales — Supplier / Logistics Growth Lane

**Status: ACTIVE / CONTROLLING**  
**Effective: 2026-09-07**  
**Owner: Casey Young**

## Purpose

Define the active supplier-growth mandate for Elevation UpScales.

Elevation is simultaneously building:

1. an actively growing ecommerce store;
2. an authorized / wholesale / dealer product-supply network;
3. dropshipping relationships that expand assortment without unnecessary inventory risk;
4. a lithium-energy logistics, freight-coordination and sourcing service;
5. specialized Hawaii / Alaska order-fulfillment capability for difficult products, including lithium / hazardous-material shipments where legally and operationally supportable.

Primary commercial positioning:

**LITHIUM ENERGY LOGISTICS + FREIGHT COORDINATION + PRODUCT SOURCING + DROPSHIPPING + ECOMMERCE + HAWAII / ALASKA FULFILLMENT**

This lane inherits `SOK_ECOMMERCE_SHIPPING_SOP.md` and `LOGISTICS_PRICING_MODEL.md`.

---

## 1. Growth objective

Build two connected commercial networks at the same time.

### Supply / ecommerce network

Seek manufacturers, distributors, importers and wholesalers that can support one or more of:

- authorized dealer / reseller accounts;
- wholesale purchasing;
- commercial purchasing;
- dropshipping / direct-to-customer fulfillment;
- project procurement;
- pallet / stocking purchases;
- distributor relationships;
- product feeds / inventory feeds where available.

### Freight / fulfillment network

Seek vendors, manufacturers and dealers that have Hawaii or Alaska demand but do not have a dependable operating path for:

- lithium batteries;
- hazardous-material / dangerous-goods shipments;
- solar equipment;
- inverters / storage systems;
- RV / off-grid products;
- palletized commercial freight;
- difficult mainland-to-Hawaii / Alaska fulfillment.

A company may qualify for one lane or both.

---

## 2. Lead qualification — evaluate every company independently across five opportunities

### A. Authorized / wholesale account

- Can Elevation become an authorized dealer, reseller, distributor, wholesale buyer or commercial customer?
- Is there a formal application or commercial contact?
- Are U.S. inventory and repeat purchasing available?

### B. Dropshipping

- Does the company explicitly support dropshipping or direct-to-customer fulfillment for approved dealers?
- Is dropshipping allowed for independent ecommerce retailers?
- Which products / brands / warehouses are eligible?
- Are blind-shipping or branded packing rules defined?
- Who owns customer service, returns, warranty and lost / damaged shipment handling?
- Are inventory feeds, order feeds, tracking feeds or portal ordering available?
- Are marketplace channels separately authorized or prohibited?
- Are dropship prices and freight commercially competitive versus stocking inventory?

Do not treat ordinary consumer shipping as a dropship program.

### C. Product / sourcing fit

Prioritize:

- solar panels / modules;
- hybrid inverters;
- inverter / chargers;
- MPPT / charge controllers;
- balance-of-system equipment;
- RV power / converter / charger systems;
- DC distribution, protection, transfer and monitoring;
- energy storage where a defined gap exists;
- off-grid appliances and practical remote-living equipment.

SOK remains Elevation's primary authorized battery partner. New battery brands are secondary unless they fill a specific product, pricing, capacity, availability, commercial or geographic gap.

### D. Hawaii / Alaska fulfillment opportunity

- Does the company exclude, restrict or manually quote Hawaii / Alaska?
- Does it receive customer / dealer demand it cannot efficiently fulfill?
- Are lithium, hazmat, DG documentation, palletization or destination receiving friction points?
- Could the vendor tender product into Elevation's mainland logistics network while Elevation manages the difficult destination path?

### E. Commercial network value

- Does the relationship improve repeat supply, pricing, regional stock access, freight origin, documentation access or product breadth?
- Does the supplier strengthen California / Colorado / Hawaii / Alaska network coverage?

## 3. Transaction structures

Elevation may support opportunities through:

### Direct Elevation product sale

Elevation sells and invoices the product and manages applicable specialized shipping.

### Supplier referral that becomes an Elevation sale

The supplier refers the Hawaii / Alaska customer or dealer inquiry to Elevation. Elevation becomes seller of record, invoices the product and manages fulfillment.

### Dropship ecommerce sale

Elevation owns the customer transaction while an approved supplier ships directly to the customer under the agreed dealer / dropship terms.

Dropshipping does not override MAP, channel authorization, product eligibility, warranty, return or marketplace restrictions.

### Third-party logistics facilitation

Another supplier / dealer owns the product transaction and engages Elevation specifically to engineer, coordinate or manage the specialized shipment.

Pricing and invoice treatment must match the actual transaction lane under `LOGISTICS_PRICING_MODEL.md`.
