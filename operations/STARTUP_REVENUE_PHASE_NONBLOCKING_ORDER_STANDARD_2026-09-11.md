# Elevation UpScales — Startup Revenue Phase Nonblocking Order Standard

**Owner:** Casey Young  
**Effective:** 2026-09-11  
**Status:** ACTIVE OWNER-AUTHORIZED OPERATING STANDARD  
**Expiration trigger:** First verified Elevation website order paid through PayPal

## Purpose

Protect startup revenue-building capacity by keeping marketplace/customer obligations moving without allowing them to outrank the work required to produce the first direct Elevation website sale.

## Controlling rule

Until the first verified Elevation website order paid through PayPal is received:

**NON-WEBSITE CUSTOMER AND MARKETPLACE ORDER ISSUES ARE LOWER-PRIORITY PARALLEL WORK, NOT COMPANY-WIDE GATES.**

This includes eBay orders/issues, TikTok marketplace orders/issues, and other customer matters that did not originate as an Elevation website order.

Accordingly:

1. Elevation website revenue activation, approved-vendor integration, catalog/store readiness, direct-site checkout/PayPal readiness, and other work materially advancing the first Elevation website order remain ahead of ordinary marketplace/customer issue work.
2. eBay orders/issues, TikTok marketplace orders/issues, and other non-Elevation-website customer matters remain serviced inside their proper Project/Worktree but at lower priority unless a controlling safety, security, legal, financial, fraud, chargeback, account-suspension, or similarly material risk requires escalation.
3. A due, late, urgent, delayed, or blocked non-website customer/order item does not stop or displace unrelated Vendor, Ecommerce, Catalog, Logistics, Operating System, RECON, Development, or revenue-building work.
4. The exact affected task may be escalated or held when necessary, but the hold applies only to that affected item/lane.
5. Workers are not automatically reassigned away from higher-value executable Worktrees merely because a marketplace/customer deadline exists.
6. Once an order originates through the Elevation website, that direct-site order is not covered by this lower-priority classification and may receive normal/high operating priority under the Master S.O.P. and current business state.
7. Existing safety, security, legal, payment, duplicate-send, destructive-action, and other protected gates remain in force.
8. The Master S.O.P. execution principle remains controlling: **HOLD ONLY THE BLOCKED ITEM → MOVE ON.**

## Expiration / transition

This temporary startup-phase standard expires when Hybrid Management verifies the **first Elevation website order paid through PayPal**.

At that point:

1. Record the verified order event and timestamp.
2. Mark this standard `EXPIRED / FIRST PAYPAL WEBSITE ORDER VERIFIED`.
3. Reconcile customer/order priority handling under the normal Master S.O.P. and current business state.
4. Expiration of this temporary standard does **not** automatically make every customer/order deadline a company-wide gate; any future global-gate treatment must come from the controlling S.O.P. or a newer explicit owner direction.

## Control phrase

**DIRECT WEBSITE REVENUE FIRST → SERVICE MARKETPLACE ISSUES IN PARALLEL → BLOCK ONLY THE AFFECTED ITEM → KEEP THE COMPANY MOVING UNTIL FIRST WEBSITE PAYPAL ORDER.**
