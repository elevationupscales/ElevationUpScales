# Elevation UpScales — Startup Revenue Phase Nonblocking Order Standard

**Owner:** Casey Young  
**Effective:** 2026-09-11  
**Status:** ACTIVE OWNER-AUTHORIZED OPERATING STANDARD  
**Expiration trigger:** First verified Elevation website order paid through PayPal

## Purpose

Keep customer/order obligations moving without allowing individual deadlines to freeze the wider company workflow during the startup revenue phase.

## Controlling rule

Until the first verified Elevation website order paid through PayPal is received:

**CUSTOMER AND ORDER DEADLINES KEEP MOVING, BUT THEY ARE NOT GLOBAL GATES.**

Accordingly:

1. Customer and order tasks remain actively serviced inside their proper Project/Worktree.
2. A due, late, urgent, delayed, or blocked customer/order item does not stop unrelated Vendor, Ecommerce, Catalog, Logistics, Operating System, RECON, Development, or other Project work.
3. The exact affected task may be prioritized, escalated, marked urgent, or held when necessary, but the hold applies only to that affected item/lane.
4. Workers are not automatically reassigned away from unrelated executable Worktrees merely because another order/customer deadline exists.
5. Existing safety, security, legal, payment, duplicate-send, destructive-action, and other protected gates remain in force.
6. The Master S.O.P. execution principle remains controlling: **HOLD ONLY THE BLOCKED ITEM → MOVE ON.**

## Expiration / transition

This temporary startup-phase standard expires when Hybrid Management verifies the **first Elevation website order paid through PayPal**.

At that point:

1. Record the verified order event and timestamp.
2. Mark this standard `EXPIRED / FIRST PAYPAL WEBSITE ORDER VERIFIED`.
3. Reconcile customer/order priority handling under the normal Master S.O.P. and current business state.
4. Expiration of this temporary standard does **not** automatically make every customer/order deadline a company-wide gate; any future global-gate treatment must come from the controlling S.O.P. or a newer explicit owner direction.

## Control phrase

**SERVICE THE ORDER → PRESERVE THE WORKTREE → BLOCK ONLY THE AFFECTED ITEM → KEEP THE COMPANY MOVING UNTIL FIRST WEBSITE PAYPAL ORDER.**
