## Product / brand fit

Olight's current product ecosystem is well aligned with Elevation's practical-use customer base.

### EDC / premium handheld

Olight's current ArkPro / ArkPro Ultra family combines compact flat-form-factor lighting with white light, UV / specialty light modes, USB-C or dual charging and rugged construction. Current public direct pricing observed at recon time is approximately $109.99 for ArkPro and $129.99 for ArkPro Ultra.

This category is a strong Elevation fit because it serves RV owners, trades, roadside / emergency kits, camping, inspection and general everyday use.

### Headlamps / hands-free work

Olight's Perun line is positioned for compact rechargeable headlamp / handheld use with magnetic mounting and high-output lighting. This is a natural attach category for RV service, off-grid maintenance, camping, vehicle work and emergency preparation.

### Clip / work lights

Olight's Oclip family is useful for hands-free work, bags, roadside visibility, automotive / inspection and camping. It gives Elevation a lower-ticket attach-sale category alongside higher-value power products.

### Bike / mobile lighting

Olight maintains dedicated bike-light products and includes Bike Lights as a dealer-application category. These broaden Elevation's outdoor / mobile-power customer reach without requiring large inventory commitment.

### Camping / emergency lighting

Olight's catalog and brand content include rechargeable area / bulb / lantern-type lighting and power-outage positioning, creating natural cross-sell opportunities with solar, batteries, RV power and backup products.
