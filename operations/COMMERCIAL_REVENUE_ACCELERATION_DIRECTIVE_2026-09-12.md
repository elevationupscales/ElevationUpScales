Every revenue lane uses this loop:

**SOURCE TRUTH → EXACT SKU → CURRENT AVAILABILITY/ORDERABILITY → CURRENT CUSTOMER PRICE/MAP → LANDED VARIABLE COST → CHANNEL COST → SAFE CONTRIBUTION → WORKING CHECKOUT → OWNED/AFFILIATE TRAFFIC → PAID ORDER → FULFILLMENT → REALIZED CONTRIBUTION → SCALE / KEEP / REPRICE / RESOURCE / STOP.**

## Priority ranking

When two products are both clean, prefer the one with the strongest combination of:

1. expected contribution dollars;
2. profit per dollar of Elevation working capital required;
3. fast cash conversion;
4. realistic demand / creator / traffic fit;
5. fulfillment reliability;
6. low refund/warranty/exception risk;
7. repeatability.

Do not chase GMV, catalog size or percentage margin by themselves.
