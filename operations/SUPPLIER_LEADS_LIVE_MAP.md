## Hard dedupe rule

Before any new supplier draft/send, classify the company as one of:

- CLEAN NEW LEAD
- EXISTING RELATIONSHIP
- EXISTING COMPANY / DIFFERENT BRANCH
- ALREADY SENT / WAITING
- DRAFT ONLY
- ROUTING FAILED
- DO NOT CONTACT

Anything other than CLEAN NEW LEAD must not be treated as fresh cold outreach.
