## Purpose

Use the structure and tone of the 2026-09-07 Renogy outreach for prospective vendors where dropshipping is a material part of the opportunity.

This is a tone and structure standard, not a copy-and-paste template. Every message must remain company-specific.

## Tone

Professional, concise, commercial, grounded and personally written.

The message should sound like Elevation is building a real supplier relationship, not asking for an affiliate link, discount code or one-off retail purchase.

## Required structure

1. **Who Elevation is**
   - Casey Young, Founder & Owner.
   - Colorado corporation / growing ecommerce operation when relevant.

2. **Why this vendor specifically**
   - Name the vendor.
   - Explain why its actual product categories fit Elevation's direction.

3. **Formal account intent**
   - State that Elevation wants an authorized dealer, reseller, wholesale, ecommerce or vendor relationship.
   - Do not frame the request as occasional retail buying.

4. **Catalog fit**
   - Identify the product families Elevation actually wants.
   - For solar/power vendors, prioritize relevant categories such as panels, kits, MPPT, DC-DC, inverter/chargers, monitoring, power management, wiring/accessories and complete RV/off-grid solutions.
   - SOK is the primary battery relationship. Do not make redundant battery recruitment the center of the email unless a defined product, price, capacity, geography or logistics gap exists.

5. **Channel discipline**
   - Ask for approved ecommerce channels.
   - Ask for MAP requirements.
   - Ask for marketplace restrictions.
   - Establish these before product activation.

6. **Dropship qualification**
   - Ask directly about approved-dealer dropship rates.
   - Ask about direct-to-customer fulfillment.
   - Ask about relevant fulfillment/account mechanics.
   - Do not assume wholesale approval includes dropship approval.

7. **First-round account questions only**
   - dealer/account application;
   - dealer pricing / partner portal;
   - approved ecommerce / marketplace rules;
   - dropship/direct fulfillment;
   - opening order or MOQ;
   - inventory/catalog data / approved media;
   - warranty/returns;
   - commercial/project pricing as volume grows.

8. **One clear next step**
   - Ask to be connected to the correct U.S. account manager or onboarding path.

## Hawaii / Alaska use

Do not force Hawaii or Alaska into every first email.

Add the logistics angle when the supplier has a credible destination gap, lost demand, lithium restriction, difficult freight problem, referral opportunity or direct strategic benefit.

When relevant, position Elevation as capable of:

- product sourcing;
- ecommerce fulfillment;
- supplier-referred order conversion;
- lithium energy logistics, freight and sourcing;
- hazardous-material / specialized logistics coordination;
- Hawaii and Alaska difficult-destination fulfillment;
- shipment-size options from small orders through pallet/LTL-LCL and larger commercial moves, subject to exact SKU/route qualification.
