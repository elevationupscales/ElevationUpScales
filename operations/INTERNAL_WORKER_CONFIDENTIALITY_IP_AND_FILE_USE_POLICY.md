# ELEVATION UPSCALES, INC. — INTERNAL WORKER CONFIDENTIALITY, INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & FILE-USE POLICY

**Status:** ACTIVE / CONTROLLING INTERNAL WORKER POLICY  
**Effective:** 2026-09-10  
**Owner:** Elevation UpScales, Inc.  
**Applies To:** employees, managers, contractors, consultants, temporary workers, AI-assisted workers, delegated agents, interns, and any other person receiving internal Elevation UpScales materials.

## 1. Purpose

This policy protects Elevation UpScales, Inc. internal data, operating methods, business records, workflows, prompts, AI work product, supplier/vendor intelligence, commercial strategy, documentation, software specifications, internal correspondence, databases, compilations, logistics methods, customer information, pricing methodology, product mappings, source materials, designs, reports, and other non-public company materials.

All such materials are collectively referred to as **Company Materials**.

## 2. Ownership and proprietary status

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Company Materials created by, for, at the direction of, or specifically for Elevation UpScales, Inc. are proprietary business assets of Elevation UpScales, Inc.

Protectable portions of Company Materials, including original content, compilations, databases, documentation, software, workflows, trade secrets, confidential information, operating procedures, designs, specifications, and derivative work product, are the intellectual property and/or proprietary confidential information of Elevation UpScales, Inc.

This policy does not claim ownership of information that is lawfully public, independently developed without use of Company Materials, or otherwise not legally protectable.

## 3. Authorized-use-only rule

Company Materials may be accessed and used only for authorized Elevation UpScales business purposes and only within the recipient's assigned scope.

Access does not transfer ownership. Possession does not grant a license for outside use.

Unless Elevation UpScales, Inc. expressly authorizes otherwise in writing, Company Materials may not be copied, reproduced, redistributed, republished, sold, licensed, transferred, disclosed, reverse-engineered, incorporated into another business system, used to train or build an outside commercial system, or reused for the benefit of another person or organization.

Limited copying reasonably necessary to perform authorized Elevation UpScales work is permitted only for the duration and scope of that work.

## 4. Confidentiality and privacy

Workers must protect non-public Company Materials from unauthorized access, disclosure, forwarding, publication, or storage.

Passwords, MFA codes, recovery codes, API keys, tokens, session cookies, tax documents, banking information, customer personal information, private vendor terms, supplier pricing, private freight rates, authentication artifacts, signed compliance records, and similar protected information must never be placed in public Git, public/shared links, unapproved AI systems, or external handoff packages.

Use the minimum necessary data for the task.

## 5. Competitive misuse restriction

A worker may not use Elevation UpScales confidential information, trade secrets, proprietary Company Materials, vendor intelligence, customer information, internal pricing methodology, logistics methods, operating procedures, or protected work product to establish, support, improve, or benefit a competing operation or to divert an Elevation UpScales business opportunity.

This is a restricted-use and confidential-information protection rule. It is not intended to prohibit lawful employment, lawful competition using public information, or use of a worker's general knowledge, training, skill, or experience where those rights cannot lawfully be restricted.

## 6. Business diversion and solicitation

To the extent permitted by applicable law, a worker may not use Company Materials or confidential information to intentionally divert Elevation UpScales customers, active vendor relationships, supplier relationships, commercial opportunities, or active business prospects for the worker's own benefit or for another organization.

## 7. AI and digital-workspace rule

Company Materials entered into or developed through ChatGPT, another AI system, automation platform, project workspace, connected application, or delegated AI worker remain subject to this policy.

AI-assisted work does not change Company ownership or the recipient's confidentiality obligations. A worker may not export Elevation's internal prompts, operating architecture, decision systems, vendor intelligence, proprietary compilations, or derivative work product for unauthorized use outside Elevation UpScales.

## 8. Return, deletion, and termination of access

Upon request, completion of an assignment, termination of access, or termination of the business relationship, the recipient must stop unauthorized use and return or securely destroy Company Materials under the recipient's control, except where retention is required by law or expressly authorized by Elevation UpScales, Inc.

Termination of access does not terminate continuing confidentiality, trade-secret, proprietary-use, or intellectual-property obligations that lawfully survive.

## 9. Legal rights and protected activity

Nothing in this policy prohibits lawful whistleblowing, reporting suspected violations of law to governmental authorities, participation in lawful investigations, compliance with subpoenas or court orders, legally protected worker communications, or other rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

## 10. File/package acceptance notice

Every internal ZIP, handoff package, worker packet, project transfer, AI continuity package, vendor-operations packet, or other downloadable package containing Company Materials must contain a conspicuous copy of the following notice at the package root and, where practical, in the package cover or README:

> **CONFIDENTIAL — PROPRIETARY PROPERTY OF ELEVATION UPSCALES, INC.**  
> This package and its contents contain confidential, proprietary, and/or intellectual-property materials of Elevation UpScales, Inc. They are provided solely for authorized Elevation UpScales business purposes. No portion may be copied, reproduced, distributed, disclosed, published, transferred, commercialized, incorporated into another business system, or reused for another person or organization without prior express written permission from Elevation UpScales, Inc.  
> **BY DOWNLOADING, ACCESSING, OPENING, EXTRACTING, RETAINING, OR USING THIS FILE OR PACKAGE AFTER RECEIVING THIS NOTICE, YOU ACKNOWLEDGE AND AGREE TO THESE TERMS OF USE AND THE ELEVATION UPSCALES, INC. INTERNAL WORKER CONFIDENTIALITY, INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & FILE-USE POLICY.**  
> If you do not agree to these terms, do not download, open, extract, retain, or use the materials and promptly delete or return them.  
> Unauthorized use, copying, disclosure, or distribution is prohibited. All rights reserved.

## 11. Future ZIP construction rule

Effective immediately, every newly generated internal Elevation UpScales ZIP or handoff package must include, at minimum:

1. `00_READ_FIRST_TERMS_OF_USE.txt` — the full acceptance notice above;
2. `INTERNAL_WORKER_CONFIDENTIALITY_IP_AND_FILE_USE_POLICY.md` — the current policy text or a complete current copy bundled into the package;
3. a short notice in the main README stating that downloading/accessing/using the package constitutes acceptance of the package terms to the fullest extent permitted by law;
4. the package creation date and controlling Company Operations baseline/commit when known;
5. no secrets or protected information merely for convenience.

If a package is transmitted by email, message, or other handoff channel, the sender should place a short version of the notice in the transmission body whenever practical so the recipient receives conspicuous notice before or at the time of access.

## 12. Worker acknowledgment and strongest-assent rule

This policy is an internal operating requirement. For workers who receive ongoing or material access to Company Materials, Elevation should also obtain a signed, click-to-accept, or otherwise recorded acknowledgment when practical.

The download/access/use notice is intended to create clear contractual notice and acceptance to the fullest extent permitted by law, but use of an additional recorded acknowledgment is preferred where available.

## 13. Enforcement

Unauthorized copying, disclosure, distribution, commercialization, misuse, or competitive use of protected Company Materials may result in revocation of access, disciplinary action, termination of the relationship, and pursuit of available contractual, intellectual-property, confidentiality, trade-secret, unfair-competition, or other legal remedies.

## 14. Priority and conflict rule

This policy is subordinate only to Casey / Owner's newer explicit direction and applicable law. It supplements `operations/MANAGEMENT_OPERATING_SOP.md` and all manager-specific SOPs.

Manager-specific instructions may add stricter handling controls but may not weaken this policy without explicit owner authorization.
