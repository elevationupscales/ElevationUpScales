## Controlling rule

This is an owner-level decision, not a lower-manager policy.

A branded item may remain listed on eBay when the exact product is sourced through Doba and the exact Doba SKU passes eBay marketplace, MAP, inventory, destination, economics and fulfillment controls.

Missing direct-manufacturer eBay authorization does **not** by itself require removing a valid Doba-backed listing.

## Catalog contraction rule after owner clarification

Do not remove a Doba-backed branded listing merely because the brand's direct relationship with Elevation lacks eBay authorization.

Use:

**EXACT DOBA SKU → EBAY ALLOWED → MAP COMPLIANT → INVENTORY → DESTINATION → LANDED COST → CONTRIBUTION → FULFILLMENT → DEMAND → KEEP / REPRICE / REBUILD / END.**

Direct manufacturer authorization is a gate only when the proposed source is **Manufacturer Direct → eBay**.

## Management implementation

All lower managers, workers and specialists must implement this owner directive as written. They may not reinterpret a pending direct-source authorization request as an order to purge otherwise valid Doba-backed listings.
