## Supplier-confirmed planning inputs

SOK's Sales Manager provided the following planning estimates and operating guidance:

- Hawaii demand historically has been small; supplier estimate is approximately two pallets annually.
- SOK reports no Hawaii warranty cases to date.
- Across SOK's broader shipment history, supplier estimates approximately 0.21% full-battery replacement and 0.4% replacement-part/component need.
- Based on sales volume, the supplier identified 206H, 314H, and 150H as the likely top warranty-activity models.
- SOK may refer Hawaii inquiries to Elevation if Elevation establishes local stock, and expects Elevation to support warranty cases for Elevation/downstream-retailer customers.
- SOK does not recommend initial spare-parts inventory; supplier stated replacement parts are held at its warehouse and can normally ship the next business day when urgent.
- Normal replenishment should occur with Elevation's next order; urgent parts can be sent separately.
- SOK proposed recognizing/crediting documented shipping cost from Elevation's Hawaii location to the approved customer for authorized warranty replacements.
- For failed batteries that cannot be repaired with parts, SOK may authorize a replacement without requiring the failed battery to return to the mainland because SOK does not currently have a Hawaii return-freight route.
- Warranty intake should include serial number, Bluetooth-app battery-parameter screenshots, and a detailed fault description.
- SOK requested that its Sales Manager be copied on warranty-case correspondence for reconciliation.

## End-of-life control

SOK asked whether a customer could simply dispose of a failed battery locally. Company Operations does not adopt an informal disposal instruction.

Current operating control:

- failed/end-of-life lithium batteries in the Hawaii business-support program must be routed through an authorized Hawaii universal-waste collector, hazardous-waste transporter, recycler, or another legally accepted destination;
- do not direct customers to household trash or ordinary municipal recycling;
- damaged/defective batteries require appropriate handling and transport controls before movement;
- final case disposition remains case-by-case based on battery condition, location, SOK authorization, and the accepting Hawaii facility.

## Initial warranty workflow

`CUSTOMER CASE → SERIAL + APP SCREENSHOTS + FAULT DETAILS → ELEVATION TRIAGE → CC SOK → SOK DIAGNOSIS/AUTHORIZATION → PART OR BATTERY REPLACEMENT → DOCUMENT LOCAL DELIVERY COST → RECONCILE CREDIT/REPLENISHMENT → APPROVED END-OF-LIFE PATH FOR FAILED BATTERY.`
