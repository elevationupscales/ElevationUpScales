# Elevation UpScales — Owner Directive: No Paid Ads Until Capital Recovery

**Status:** ACTIVE / OWNER CONTROL  
**Date:** 2026-09-12 MDT  
**Owner:** Casey Young  
**Authority:** Newest explicit owner direction; supersedes any lower-level workflow that could be read to permit paid acquisition before recovery.

## Owner direction

**Elevation UpScales does not run paid ads until the company is out of the recovery hole.**

This is a company-wide cash-protection rule, not a product-by-product advertising decision.

## Current control

Until capital recovery is achieved and the Owner explicitly reopens paid acquisition:

- **NO paid social ads**;
- **NO boosted posts**;
- **NO paid search / PPC**;
- **NO sponsored marketplace traffic purchased by Elevation**;
- **NO paid retargeting or other prepaid media acquisition**;
- a high product margin does **not** override this rule.

Allowed to continue under existing lane controls:

- organic social;
- owned website/store traffic;
- SEO/content/discoverability;
- email and direct outreach;
- free marketplace exposure;
- performance-based creator/affiliate activity where there is no upfront ad spend and the resulting order remains positive-contribution;
- other no-upfront-spend traffic already authorized by the owning lane.

This directive does not automatically authorize samples, giveaways, discounts, creator retainers, or other marketing cash expenditures. Those remain subject to their existing contribution/cash controls and owner approval where required.

## Recovery gate

Do not infer that paid ads are reopened merely because one product is profitable, marketplace receivables are released, or one cash balance becomes positive.

The paid-ad gate opens only after:

1. the current business capital-recovery deficit / "hole" is closed under the controlling profitability records; and
2. the Owner explicitly authorizes paid acquisition again.

Until both are true, all workers and managers must classify paid acquisition as **BLOCKED BY OWNER CAPITAL-RECOVERY RULE**.

## Interaction with current product programs

- Renogy Adventurer 30A may remain a strong organic/order-first hero, but **no paid ads**.
- Renogy RBM500 remains free/owned testing only unless a newer owner directive changes the traffic rule.
- VEVOR first-sale products remain free/owned traffic only.
- Fourthwall startup-capital revenue should continue through organic/owned/social-commerce/creator paths that do not require prepaid media spend.
- TikTok seller-account appeal remains separate and is not reopened by this directive.

## Replay guard

Any document or worker note saying a SKU is "eligible for paid acquisition," "paid-ad ready," or similar before capital recovery must be read as **economics capability only, not execution authority**. This Owner Directive controls execution.

**CONTROL PHRASE:**

**OUT OF THE HOLE FIRST → ORGANIC / OWNED TRAFFIC NOW → PROTECT CASH → REALIZE PROFIT → CLOSE CAPITAL-RECOVERY GAP → OWNER REOPENS PAID ADS.**
