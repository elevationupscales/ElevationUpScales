## Recon objective

This pass follows the current company posture: revenue conversion first, opportunistic prospecting only when a prospect clearly strengthens Elevation's existing lithium / solar / RV / off-grid / outdoor / difficult-destination direction.

This second wave intentionally concentrates on:

- outdoor and work lighting that fits the brand direction established by the Olight interest;
- RV / vehicle battery charging and emergency power accessories that attach naturally to SOK and Renogy;
- communications / preparedness products;
- DC electrical / BOS sourcing that can help resolve existing sourcing gaps;
- Western / Colorado suppliers where physical proximity improves relationship or sourcing value.

Gmail domain-level dedupe checks found no existing direct correspondence for the prospects listed below at the time of this recon. GitHub search also found no existing supplier-state record for these names. They remain **prospects only** until management approves any next action.

# 4. Midland Radio

## PROSPECT
**Category:** Vendor / Manufacturer  
**Current relationship state:** CLEAN NEW PROSPECT

## WHY IT FITS ELEVATION
Midland adds emergency preparedness, RV/overland and outdoor communications rather than another redundant battery line. Products include two-way radios, weather-alert equipment and communications accessories.

## VERIFIED OPPORTUNITY
Midland has a formal **Authorized Seller Program** and UPP pricing policy. Authorization is required for resale; approved dealers receive dealer pricing, stock access, marketing resources, warranty support and priority support. Midland states the dealer review process can take 4–6 weeks.

Midland also states its own normal fulfillment is limited to the 48 contiguous states and select Alaska areas; customers in Hawaii and other outlying U.S. areas are directed to third-party authorized sellers. This creates a real destination-market angle, but it is not yet permission for Elevation to advertise island fulfillment.

## COMMERCIAL RISKS
Channel-specific ecommerce/marketplace permissions must be confirmed. Midland's consumer-site terms prohibit consumer purchases for resale, so Elevation must use the authorized dealer route only.
