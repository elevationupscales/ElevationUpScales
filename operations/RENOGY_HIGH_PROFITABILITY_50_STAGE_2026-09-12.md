Protected dealer cost values are intentionally **not recorded in Git**. They are stored in the private Shopify inventory-item cost field for operational contribution analysis.

- private Shopify unit cost populated from the approved dealer sheet;
- Renogy vendor assignment;
- Lower-48 / Direct-Site classification;
- `Profitability Prequalified` tag;
- `Price Verification Required` tag;
- `Inventory Verification Required` tag;
- inventory policy DENY while in verification;
- no synthetic inventory quantity.

## Why these are not auto-activated

Renogy's approved dealer terms require Elevation's public advertised price to remain below Renogy's current website price. Current public price, exact orderability/inventory, delayed-order authority where needed, and positive final contribution must therefore be verified SKU-by-SKU before storefront activation.

**DRAFT is a verification state, not a rejection.** Missing data on one SKU does not block cleaner SKUs.
