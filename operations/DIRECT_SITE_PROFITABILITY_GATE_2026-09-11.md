# Elevation UpScales — Direct-Site Profitability Gate

**Status:** ACTIVE / OWNER-DIRECTED REVENUE CONTROL  
**Effective:** 2026-09-11  
**Owner:** Casey Young  
**Applies To:** Elevation direct-site / Shopify / PayPal product promotion, pricing, first-sale acceleration, vendor catalog activation and order economics

## Owner rule

Elevation is not pursuing sales for sales-volume alone.

**MAKE SALES → MAKE PROFIT → PROTECT CASH → REPEAT WHAT WORKS.**

A product is not a preferred promotion candidate merely because it is inexpensive, likely to convert, currently published, or capable of reaching checkout.

The current startup objective is the **first profitable Elevation direct-site order**, followed by repeatable profitable orders.

## Profitability gate

Before an item is actively promoted as a first-sale / revenue candidate, establish enough current facts to show that the order is expected to produce **positive contribution** under the intended checkout and fulfillment path.

Use this working calculation:

**CUSTOMER PRODUCT REVENUE**  
**+ CUSTOMER-PAID SHIPPING REVENUE, IF ANY**  
**− CURRENT SUPPLIER PRODUCT COST**  
**− SUPPLIER SHIPPING / FREIGHT PAID BY ELEVATION**  
**− PAYMENT-PROCESSOR / CHECKOUT FEES ACTUALLY APPLICABLE**  
**− SHOPIFY / PLATFORM PER-ORDER FEES ACTUALLY APPLICABLE**  
**− DISCOUNTS / CREDITS FUNDED BY ELEVATION**  
**− PACKAGING / HANDLING / FULFILLMENT VARIABLE COSTS PAID BY ELEVATION**  
**− OTHER KNOWN ORDER-SPECIFIC VARIABLE COSTS**  
**= ORDER CONTRIBUTION**

Sales tax collected for remittance is not treated as product revenue or profit.

If paid advertising is used for a campaign, attributable customer-acquisition spend must also be considered when judging whether the campaign is actually profitable. The current first-sale plan should prefer free/owned traffic unless paid acquisition is separately approved and economics are known.

## Default decision states

### `PROMOTE`
Use only when:

- exact SKU/source identity is verified;
- current supplier sellability/orderability is verified;
- customer price complies with MAP / price controls;
- material variable costs are known enough to calculate order economics;
- expected order contribution is **greater than $0**;
- fulfillment path is executable;
- checkout path is working.

### `HOLD — ECONOMICS UNKNOWN`
Use when a material current cost is unknown, including supplier cost, freight, transaction treatment or another order-specific variable cost that could make the sale unprofitable.

### `HOLD — NEGATIVE CONTRIBUTION`
Use when current expected order contribution is $0 or below.

### `OWNER REVIEW — STRATEGIC EXCEPTION`
A deliberate loss leader, customer-acquisition subsidy, bundle subsidy, launch promotion or other below-normal-margin strategy requires Casey's explicit approval. Automation and vendor workers may not create or normalize loss-making sales on their own.

## Ranking rule

Promotion ranking is not simply:

**LOWEST PRICE FIRST.**

Rank clean candidates using:

**CUSTOMER DEMAND / PURCHASE FRICTION + EXPECTED DOLLAR CONTRIBUTION + EXPECTED CONTRIBUTION RATE + BRAND FIT + FULFILLMENT RELIABILITY + SUPPORT/RETURN RISK.**

A $35 item with almost no contribution can rank below a $65 item that still converts easily and produces materially better profit.

Do not invent a fixed percentage-margin target until the owner establishes one or enough real order data exists to set a rational target by category/vendor. Until then, **positive contribution is the minimum gate, not the performance target**.

## Price / discount control

- Never discount below MAP or another controlling supplier price floor.
- Never create a discount merely to obtain the first sale when it makes expected contribution non-positive.
- Customer-paid shipping may be used where lawful and appropriate, but do not disguise product economics through unsupported shipping charges.
- Free shipping is not automatically free to Elevation; include any supplier/freight cost paid by Elevation.
- Supplier promotional pricing must be current and must not be assumed permanent.

## Order-time recheck

For vendor-fulfilled goods, reverify the exact SKU's current source, sellability and current supplier cost before placing the supplier order when the vendor lane requires live revalidation.

If a paid customer order unexpectedly becomes negative because the supplier cost, shipping, availability or other economics changed after checkout:

**ROUTE THE EXACT ORDER AS AN ECONOMIC EXCEPTION → PROTECT THE CUSTOMER OBLIGATION → DO NOT NORMALIZE THE LOSS-MAKING PATH → CORRECT PRICE/SOURCE/PROMOTION STATE BEFORE THE NEXT ORDER.**

Do not use profitability as a reason to ignore an already-created lawful customer obligation.
