## PURPOSE

Lock the current negotiation posture for the developing SOK Hawaii warranty-support model without prematurely committing Elevation to a permanent commercial structure.

## CURRENT DIRECTIVE

Elevation should establish that it is building the Hawaii storage, inventory, and fulfillment capacity needed to support SOK-authorized warranty replacements locally.

The next supplier conversation should be focused on obtaining a small set of planning inputs needed to size a workable system.

Do not present the full internal commercial model, multi-vendor Warranty Fulfillment strategy, warehouse economics, future service-fee structure, margin model, or negotiating range while those items remain under internal development.

## EXTERNAL POSTURE TO SOK

Communicate that Elevation is prepared to continue building the Hawaii warranty-support capability and wants to size the first operating model around realistic SOK volume and support requirements.

Ask only for the operating inputs that materially affect the first model, including:

- approximate Hawaii warranty / replacement / support volume;
- which models or replacement parts create the most warranty activity;
- SOK's recommended replacement-stock method or reserve level for a dealer supporting an area locally;
- expected replenishment timing after Elevation uses locally held stock for an authorized replacement;
- clarification of how SOK's stated shipping-cost deduction / freight support should work for Hawaii warranty replacements.

Estimates are acceptable where exact historical figures are unavailable.

## INTERNAL HOLD

While waiting for SOK's response, keep the following internal:

- permanent warranty-stock quantity;
- warehouse and freight cost model;
- service pricing / compensation concepts;
- multi-vendor Warranty Fulfillment expansion;
- negotiating thresholds and counteroffer structure;
- profitability assumptions;
- long-term Hawaii regional-support structure.

Do not negotiate permanent economics from guesses.

## DECISION SEQUENCE

**CAPACITY ESTABLISHED → REQUEST SOK OPERATING INPUTS → HOLD COMMERCIAL MODEL INTERNAL → RECEIVE RESPONSE → MODEL TRUE COST / VALUE → FORM COMMERCIAL COUNTERPROPOSAL**

## BOUNDARIES

This directive does not authorize:

- reserve-stock purchase;
- warehouse minimum or long-term commitment;
- guaranteed warranty volume;
- guaranteed replacement SLA;
- standing Elevation absorption of Hawaii warranty freight;
- service-fee or retainer terms;
- exclusivity;
- disclosure of other supplier relationships or internal Warranty Fulfillment strategy.

Those remain owner gates where applicable.
