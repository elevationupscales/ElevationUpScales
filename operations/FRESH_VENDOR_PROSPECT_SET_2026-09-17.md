# Elevation UpScales — Fresh Vendor Prospect Set

**Status:** OWNER-DIRECTED RESEARCH / FRESH PROSPECTS  
**Effective:** 2026-09-17  
**Management source:** GitHub `/operations/`  
**Purpose:** Add a focused prospect set aligned with the current revenue, dropship, solar/off-grid, and Hawaii/Alaska logistics direction without turning broad vendor acquisition back into the primary company mission.

## Direction used for this pass

Current company posture is revenue conversion first. This prospect set therefore favors suppliers that can add one or more of the following without duplicating SOK's primary battery role:

- authorized dealer / direct reseller opportunity;
- dropship or direct-to-customer fulfillment;
- portable / whole-home backup products that are distinct from standalone battery inventory;
- solar panels, mobile power electronics, racking and BOS;
- RV / off-grid / marine power products that attach naturally to SOK;
- a documented Hawaii / Alaska shipping gap that Elevation may be able to solve through product resale, supplier referral, or specialized logistics;
- California / Western U.S. inventory or operating presence that improves sourcing and freight positioning.

No external email is authorized by this file. Channel, MAP, marketplace, warranty, return, and dropship approval remain separate commercial gates.

## 1. BLUETTI — A+ / dealer + dropship + island-gap lead

**Why it fits**
- Official wholesale/partnership program actively seeks dealers.
- BLUETTI states that partners may choose **drop shipping or wholesale**.
- It states it holds inventory in overseas/U.S.-serving warehouse channels, pays last-mile shipping in dropship mode, and handles after-sales service.
- Its U.S. shipping policy explicitly excludes Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, U.S. Virgin Islands and Guam.
- Product fit includes portable power stations, expandable home backup and solar products.

**Best Elevation lane**
Dealer / dropship ecommerce + Hawaii/Alaska fulfillment + supplier-referral logistics.

**Opening angle**
Elevation can sell approved BLUETTI products through its direct ecommerce channel while separately exploring a compliant fulfillment path for island demand that BLUETTI currently declines through standard U.S. shipping.

**Commercial contact / path**
Wholesale & Partnership Inquiry; U.S./Canada/Australia route: `service@bluettipower.com`.

**Activation gate**
Need exact approved channels, MAP, marketplace restrictions, eligible dropship SKUs, U.S. fulfillment origin, warranty/RMA responsibilities and written permission for any Elevation-managed Hawaii/Alaska movement.

**Public sources**
- https://www.bluettipower.com/pages/wholesale
- https://www.bluettipower.com/pages/shipping-policy/

## 2. Jackery — A+ / direct dealer + lithium-logistics fit

**Why it fits**
- Official reseller application is live.
- The application explicitly asks whether the applicant can ship batteries larger than 300Wh, making Elevation's lithium-logistics capability directly relevant to dealer qualification.
- Jackery states that dealers may not sell on Amazon/eBay, which makes direct-site channel discipline important but does not automatically disqualify Elevation's Shopify/direct-web model.
- Jackery's U.S. shipping policy excludes Hawaii, Alaska, Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands because lithium generators move by ground in the contiguous U.S.

**Best Elevation lane**
Authorized direct-site dealer + Hawaii/Alaska difficult-destination fulfillment.

**Opening angle**
Apply as a disciplined direct-site reseller with an established lithium-freight workflow rather than as a marketplace arbitrage seller.

**Commercial contact / path**
Official `Become Reseller` application.

**Activation gate**
Need written confirmation that Elevation's direct website is approved, exact MAP/advertising rules, which products are dealer-eligible, whether dropship is available, and how Jackery wants island orders/warranty handled.

**Public sources**
- https://www.jackery.com/pages/become-reseller
- https://www.jackery.com/policies/shipping-policy

## 3. Goal Zero — A+ / authorized-dealer + explicit lithium island gap

**Why it fits**
- Goal Zero operates an authorized dealer portal with `Direct Dealer - New`, indirect dealer and distributor classifications.
- Goal Zero's published FAQ states it has a dealer program and routes U.S. dealer inquiries to `USsales@goalzero.com`.
- Goal Zero explicitly states that it cannot ship lithium products over 100Wh directly to Alaska, Hawaii or Puerto Rico.
- Its own support guidance says authorized retailers in those markets receive inventory by palletized or containerized freight, which is directly aligned with Elevation's specialized logistics model.

**Best Elevation lane**
Authorized dealer + island replenishment / referral fulfillment + portable backup ecommerce.

**Opening angle**
Goal Zero already recognizes pallet/container movement as the practical island solution. Elevation can approach as a dealer candidate that also understands and can coordinate that freight model.

**Commercial contact / path**
`USsales@goalzero.com` plus the authorized dealer portal.

**Activation gate**
Dealer approval, channel rules, MAP, warranty obligations for island customers, direct vs distributor purchasing, and written logistics handling boundaries.

**Public sources**
- https://dealer.goalzero.com/Register
- https://help.goalzero.com/hc/en-us/articles/45357607139867-Do-you-ship-lithium-power-stations-to-Alaska-Hawaii-or-Puerto-Rico
