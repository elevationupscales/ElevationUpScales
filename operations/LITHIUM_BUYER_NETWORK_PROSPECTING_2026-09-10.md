## Hawaii Emphasis

Hawaii is the most important market-development lane.

Prospecting should prioritize organizations that could benefit from:

- a dependable lithium supply relationship
- local or controlled Hawaii availability
- consolidated freight or batch purchasing
- recurring dealer/reseller orders
- solar/off-grid system integration
- RV, marine, backup-power, or remote-power demand

Do not promise Hawaii freight economics, delivery timing, exclusivity, standing inventory, or universal product eligibility unless those facts are separately verified by Operations.

## Prospect Record Standard

Each prospect profile should capture, when reasonably available:

- company / organization name
- city / island / region
- website
- buyer type / segment
- likely lithium use case
- estimated demand signal: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / UNKNOWN
- likely battery class: 12V / 24V / 48V / mixed / unknown
- likely purchase behavior: end-user / installer / reseller / dealer / fleet / project buyer
- relevant brands or battery products currently visible, if public
- contact route: purchasing / owner / operations / sales / general inquiry
- public phone/email/contact form where appropriate
- fit for SOK / future multi-supplier catalog / unknown
- Hawaii freight relevance where applicable
- outreach readiness: READY / NEEDS RESEARCH / HOLD
- evidence/source link
- next action

## Qualification Rules

A prospect is more valuable when there is evidence of one or more of the following:

- recurring installation or resale activity
- lithium batteries already sold, specified, installed, or discussed
- RV, marine, solar, off-grid, backup-power, fleet, or remote-power specialization
- geographic/logistics friction that Elevation can potentially solve
- repeat commercial demand rather than one-off consumer interest
- clear purchasing or partnership contact path

Do not treat a generic directory listing as a qualified lead without further evidence.
