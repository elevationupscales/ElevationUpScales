## Fulfillment finding

The highest-risk operational finding from the pass is **tracking and fulfillment reliability**.

A proven-selling listing lost value when a buyer experienced prolonged tracking inactivity and the order was later refunded.

Therefore:

**A WINNING LISTING IS NOT A WINNING SYSTEM UNTIL FULFILLMENT IS RELIABLE.**

Company Operations should prioritize order release, supplier confirmation, tracking capture, late-order escalation, and source fallback on proven products before expanding random catalog volume.

## Catalog / listing control

The reconciliation supports the current cleanup posture:

- preserve listings with useful sales history, views, watchers, or demonstrated demand;
- repair pricing, title, images, shipping, source, and fulfillment in place where viable;
- do not blanket-delete the eBay catalog;
- end or replace listings only when economics, supply reliability, policy risk, or demand evidence does not justify preservation;
- paid orders and customer fulfillment outrank catalog cleanup;
- use proven demand to guide sourcing instead of adding unrelated volume.
