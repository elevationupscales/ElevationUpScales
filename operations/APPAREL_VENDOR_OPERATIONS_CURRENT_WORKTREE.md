## Profitability formula

For every creator-promoted SKU:

**NET CONTRIBUTION = CUSTOMER REVENUE - PROVIDER COST - SHIPPING SUBSIDY - PLATFORM/PAYMENT FEES - AFFILIATE COMMISSION - PROMO/DISCOUNT - SAMPLE/ACQUISITION ALLOCATION - SELLER-FUNDED AFTERSALE COSTS.**

Do not set commission by instinct alone.

### Required per-SKU values

- exact provider and variant;
- selling price;
- provider/base cost;
- shipping model;
- channel/payment fees;
- affiliate commission % + $;
- sample/refundable sample exposure;
- discount/promo exposure;
- expected contribution $ + %;
- realized contribution $ + % once sold.

## Creator profitability bands

Use SKU-specific math; these are operating bands, not automatic promises:

- `CORE PROFIT` — creator offer leaves target net contribution intact.
- `AGGRESSIVE ACQUISITION` — higher commission justified by strong base margin and expected creator volume; still positive contribution unless Casey explicitly approves acquisition spend.
- `TEST ONLY` — bounded creator/sample exposure while conversion is unproven.
- `HOLD` — current price/provider cannot safely support affiliate commission.

Existing historical 20% creator commission is a useful starting reference, not a universal floor or ceiling. Recalculate by SKU before scaling.

## Product buckets

### CORE HERO

3–5 products with strong brand fit, margin, fulfillment reliability and creator appeal.

### VIRAL TEST

Short-lived products selected from trend signals and creator demand. Test small, measure quickly, kill weak economics quickly.

### FEMALE CREATOR / LIFESTYLE

Maintain a dedicated rotating lane for products such as:

- women's cropped tees/hoodies;
- jogger/short sets;
- fitted/lifestyle apparel;
- hats/caps with fashion appeal;
- bags/hip bags/crossbody accessories;
- seasonal viral apparel opportunities.

Selection is based on creator/demand fit **and** contribution after affiliate economics.

### HOLD / REPRICE / REROUTE

Attractive products with inadequate current economics. Compare provider alternatives before retiring a good design.
