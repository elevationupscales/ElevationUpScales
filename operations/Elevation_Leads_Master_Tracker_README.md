# Elevation Leads Master Tracker

The canonical leads spreadsheet is `operations/Elevation_Leads_Master_Tracker_2026-09-17.xlsx`.

Use it to track current lead priority, relationship state, management-review requirement, outreach/contact state, Hawaii opportunity, dropship status, next management action, and contact history.

## Control

**Research is not outreach. Draft is not send. Management approval controls external contact.**

Before changing outreach status to `SENT`, the exact external action must have management approval under the current Leads Prospector Manager control.

The workbook is public-safe. Do not add private pricing, tax identifiers, legal packets, raw correspondence, carrier rates, credentials, customer PII, or other protected commercial records.

Update `Master Leads` first and append material contact/reply events to `Contact Log`.
