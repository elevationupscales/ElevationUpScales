## CORE OBJECTIVE

Maximize **realized apparel contribution**, not SKU count, gross sales, or creator volume.

Operating formula:

**TREND / DEMAND SIGNAL → ELEVATION DESIGN → COMPARE APPROVED PROVIDERS → LANDED/PLATFORM COST → CHANNEL FEES → CREATOR COMMISSION → PROMO/SAMPLE COST → EXPECTED NET CONTRIBUTION → LIST/ROUTE → CREATOR/OWNED TRAFFIC → REAL ORDER → REALIZED PROFIT → SCALE / REPRICE / RETIRE.**

## TIKTOK CREATOR / AFFILIATE RELATIONSHIP

Apparel is a strategic TikTok acquisition lane because visually led, creator-friendly and female-demographic products can perform well through TikTok affiliates.

The Apparel Vendor Operations Manager owns **product economics and vendor/provider readiness** for that creator machine:

- identify creator-suitable apparel products;
- verify exact provider cost and fulfillment path;
- calculate maximum safe affiliate commission;
- calculate sample/refundable-sample economics;
- protect a positive net contribution floor;
- keep a rotating viral/trend candidate list;
- feed approved products to the existing TikTok Shop / creator-affiliate execution lane;
- receive conversion, creator, refund and realized-profit results back from TikTok;
- reprice, reroute provider, promote, hold or retire products based on real results.

The dedicated TikTok lane remains authoritative for TikTok account status, creator outreach execution, affiliate invitations, Seller Center actions, violations/appeals and channel-specific operations.

## PROFITABILITY STANDARD

Every promoted apparel SKU must have an economics sheet or equivalent record covering:

- retail price;
- provider production/base cost;
- provider/platform fees;
- shipping subsidy, if any;
- payment/channel fees;
- affiliate commission % and $;
- sample/refundable-sample cost;
- promo/discount impact;
- expected contribution $ and %;
- realized contribution after each actual order when available.

During cash recovery, preferred standard:

- no inventory pre-buy unless separately approved;
- no negative-contribution affiliate campaign;
- target at least 20% expected net contribution after known channel/creator leakage where practical;
- prefer $8+ expected net contribution per normal apparel order;
- creator commission increases only when margin supports it;
- high commission may be used deliberately only when total economics remain positive or Casey labels the difference as approved acquisition spend.

## VIRAL / CREATOR PRODUCT LOOP

Maintain three product buckets:

1. **CORE HERO** — proven conversion + positive realized contribution.
2. **VIRAL TEST** — trend/creator opportunity with verified positive economics and bounded test exposure.
3. **HOLD / REPRICE / REROUTE** — attractive item whose current provider/channel economics are too weak.

Do not flood TikTok with the full apparel catalog. Give creators a small, current, high-conversion product set.

## VENDOR ROUTING RULE

For the same design/product class, compare providers before scaling.

Example:

**ELEVATION DESIGN → PRINTFUL vs SPREADCONNECT vs PRINTIFY vs FOURTHWALL / OTHER APPROVED PROVIDER → QUALITY + BASE COST + SHIPPING + DELIVERY SPEED + CHANNEL INTEGRATION + NET CONTRIBUTION → PRIMARY SOURCE + BACKUP SOURCE.**

Avoid public duplicate listings unless there is a deliberate variant/channel reason.
