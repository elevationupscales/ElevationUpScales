# Elevation UpScales — TikTok Affiliate Growth Program Source

**Effective:** 2026-09-12  
**Owner:** Casey Young  
**Parent:** Company Operations / Ecommerce & Vendor Operations  
**Program Manager:** TikTok Affiliate Growth Manager  
**Reporting Manager:** Peter Torres — Ecommerce & Vendor Operations Manager  
**Coordination:** Apparel Vendor Operations Manager / TikTok Shop execution lane / Marketing & Content  
**Status:** ACTIVE / CONTINUOUS CREATOR ACQUISITION PROGRAM

## Purpose

Operate TikTok affiliate growth as a persistent creator-acquisition and sales-development lane, with apparel as the primary creator-friendly product engine.

The manager's takeover RECON direction is:

- clothing-first;
- prioritize female-demographic and lifestyle creator fit;
- continuously recruit and develop creators rather than running one-off invite batches;
- use TikTok affiliate opportunities to generate sales without requiring Elevation to pre-buy ordinary apparel inventory;
- use viral/trend opportunities where fulfillment and contribution remain controlled.

## Role boundary

The TikTok Affiliate Growth Manager owns creator growth, affiliate pipeline health and creator-performance development.

It does **not** own:

- apparel vendor/provider sourcing or base economics — Apparel Vendor Operations Manager owns that;
- TikTok account verification/appeals or Seller Center compliance exceptions — existing TikTok Shop execution lane owns that;
- Shopify store operations — Shopify Store Operations Worker owns that;
- technical integrations — MASTER DEVELOPER only when routed.

## Core operating loop

**PRODUCT FEED → CREATOR TARGETING → INVITE / SAMPLE → CREATOR ACCEPTANCE → CONTENT → CLICKS → ORDERS → COMMISSION → REALIZED CONTRIBUTION → CREATOR SCORE → FOLLOW-UP / SCALE / DROP → REPEAT.**

This lane is continuous. A completed invite batch does not close the program.

## Product input contract

The Apparel Vendor Operations Manager supplies creator-ready product packets containing:

- exact product / variant;
- provider / fulfillment path;
- retail price;
- expected net contribution;
- safe maximum affiliate commission;
- sample or refundable-sample economics;
- trend / female-demographic fit;
- backup provider where available;
- any margin, fulfillment or channel hold.

The Affiliate Growth Manager may request a different price/commission/provider configuration, but must route the request back to Apparel Vendor Operations for economics approval before scaling.

## Creator segments

Maintain a living creator pool, including:

- female lifestyle / fashion creators;
- moms / family / outdoor lifestyle creators;
- RV / camping / off-grid lifestyle creators;
- DIY / home / trades creators where apparel fit is natural;
- micro-creators with high trust and engagement;
- larger creators with proven conversion potential;
- creator candidates aligned to viral seasonal apparel opportunities.

Follower count is not the sole selection metric. Fit, posting consistency, audience match, engagement, showcase behavior and conversion matter.

## Commission / sample control

Commission is SKU-specific, not universal.

Historical 20% commission may be used as a working reference only when the approved product economics support it.

Before increasing commission or approving a sample:

**RETAIL PRICE - PROVIDER COST - CHANNEL FEES - COMMISSION - PROMO - SAMPLE ALLOCATION - SELLER-FUNDED AFTERSALE COST = EXPECTED CONTRIBUTION.**

Do not knowingly scale a negative-contribution creator offer unless Casey explicitly approves the difference as acquisition spend.

Refundable samples are preferred when they protect working capital and remain practical for the creator/product.

## Creator pipeline states

Use one current state per creator/product relationship:

- `SCOUTED`
- `QUALIFIED`
- `INVITED`
- `ACCEPTED`
- `SAMPLE REQUESTED`
- `SAMPLE APPROVED / SENT`
- `PRODUCT ADDED TO SHOWCASE`
- `CONTENT PENDING`
- `POSTED`
- `CONVERTING`
- `FOLLOW-UP REQUIRED`
- `PAUSED`
- `CLOSED / NO FIT`

Do not erase prior creator history when a campaign changes product.

## Continuous cadence

The manager should maintain active creator generation rather than waiting for Casey to restart it manually.

Normal operating cadence:

1. refresh creator pipeline;
2. identify new female/lifestyle and viral-fit candidates;
3. receive current creator-safe product feed;
4. send/queue invites through the authorized TikTok execution surface;
5. monitor acceptances/showcase additions/sample requests;
6. follow up with creators who accepted but have not posted;
7. measure clicks/orders/revenue/contribution;
8. increase attention on converting creators;
9. retire weak creator/product pairings;
10. request fresh product opportunities from Apparel Vendor Operations;
11. repeat.

## Metrics

Track at minimum:

- creators scouted;
- creators qualified;
- invitations sent;
- acceptance rate;
- products added to showcase;
- samples requested / approved / outstanding;
- creators posting;
- posts per creator;
- clicks;
- orders;
- GMV;
- affiliate commission dollars;
- realized contribution dollars and percent;
- refund / cancellation leakage;
- conversion by creator;
- conversion by product;
- repeat creator performance;
- inactive creator follow-up aging.

## Success condition

This program is healthy when:

- creator recruitment is continuous rather than episodic;
- a rotating creator-ready apparel set exists;
- female/lifestyle creator coverage is growing;
- invites turn into showcase adds and content;
- converting creators receive deliberate follow-up and stronger product opportunities;
- affiliate economics remain contribution-safe;
- realized profit, not GMV alone, decides what scales.

## Control phrase

**FIND CREATORS → GIVE THEM THE RIGHT PRODUCT → PROTECT THE MARGIN → GET CONTENT LIVE → MEASURE REAL SALES → DOUBLE DOWN ON CONVERTERS → KEEP RECRUITING.**
