# Elevation UpScales — TikTok Affiliate Growth Program Source

**Effective:** 2026-09-12  
**Owner:** Casey Young  
**Parent:** Company Operations / Ecommerce & Vendor Operations  
**Program Manager:** TikTok Affiliate Growth Manager  
**Reporting Manager:** Peter Torres — Ecommerce & Vendor Operations Manager  
**Coordination:** Apparel Vendor Operations Manager / approved Outdoor-Camping product owners / TikTok Shop execution lane / Marketing & Content  
**Status:** ACTIVE / CONTINUOUS CREATOR ACQUISITION PROGRAM

## Purpose

Operate TikTok affiliate growth as a persistent creator-acquisition and sales-development lane across the products that best fit TikTok commerce.

TikTok is currently product-restricted, so creator commerce operates as a **mixed eligible-product lane**. When TikTok restrictions are lifted and catalog breadth supports it, the affiliate program will separate into two primary product lanes without creating duplicate account management:

1. **APPAREL / LIFESTYLE**
2. **CAMPING / OUTDOOR GEAR**

Both lanes remain under one TikTok Affiliate Growth Manager and one creator-performance system.

## Owner direction

- Apparel remains a major TikTok growth engine because it fits creator-led visual selling and female/lifestyle demographics.
- Camping and outdoor gear are also strategic affiliate products and must be actively used where TikTok eligibility, fulfillment and economics allow.
- Viral/trending opportunities should be exploited quickly when they are sellable, fulfillable and margin-safe.
- Creator recruitment must run continuously rather than through one-off invite batches.
- Warm creators must be cultivated, not abandoned after the initial invite/showcase step.
- TikTok affiliate growth is expected to become a repeatable revenue engine and should operate like a disciplined pipeline, not an occasional marketing experiment.

## Role boundary

The TikTok Affiliate Growth Manager owns:

- creator discovery and qualification;
- creator pipeline health;
- invitations / affiliate relationship follow-up through authorized TikTok surfaces;
- showcase-add and sample follow-up;
- creator-product matching;
- creator performance scoring;
- continuous recruitment cadence;
- product-demand feedback to product/vendor owners;
- affiliate growth metrics and realized-contribution feedback.

It does **not** own:

- apparel provider sourcing/base economics — Apparel Vendor Operations Manager owns that;
- camping/outdoor supplier authorization/base economics — the applicable vendor/project owner owns that;
- TikTok seller verification/appeals or account-compliance exceptions — existing TikTok Shop execution lane owns that;
- Shopify store operations — Shopify Store Operations Worker owns that;
- technical integrations — MASTER DEVELOPER only when routed.

## Current vs future channel architecture

### CURRENT — RESTRICTIONS ACTIVE

Operate one mixed TikTok affiliate catalog using only products currently eligible/available in the store.

Do not force artificial category separation if TikTok currently limits the catalog. Instead tag every affiliate product internally as:

- `APPAREL`
- `OUTDOOR_CAMPING`
- `OTHER_APPROVED_TEST`

Creator targeting and profitability still remain product-specific.

### FUTURE — RESTRICTIONS LIFTED

Split product merchandising and creator targeting into two primary lanes:

#### Apparel / Lifestyle

Focus on:

- women's apparel;
- cropped tees / hoodies;
- coordinated sets;
- hats / caps;
- bags / crossbody / hip bags;
- lifestyle basics;
- creator capsules;
- viral seasonal apparel.

Primary audiences include female lifestyle/fashion, moms/family lifestyle, outdoor lifestyle and creator-led trend communities.

#### Camping / Outdoor Gear

Focus on:

- RV/camping convenience products;
- outdoor tools/accessories;
- campsite organization;
- portable power-support accessories where TikTok eligibility allows;
- hiking/travel/outdoor utility products;
- seasonal camping products;
- viral outdoor utility products.

Primary audiences include camping/RV, overlanding, outdoor lifestyle, DIY, family travel, hunting/fishing-adjacent where product/category rules allow, and practical-gear creators.

This category split is a merchandising/creator-growth structure only. It does not create separate TikTok accounts or duplicate Affiliate Growth managers.

## Core operating loop

**PRODUCT FEED → PRODUCT LANE → CREATOR TARGETING → INVITE / SAMPLE → CREATOR ACCEPTANCE → SHOWCASE / CONTENT → CLICKS → ORDERS → COMMISSION → REALIZED CONTRIBUTION → CREATOR SCORE → FOLLOW-UP / SCALE / DROP → REPEAT.**

A completed invite batch does not close the program.

The Affiliate Growth Manager may request a different price, commission, product or source configuration, but scaling requires approval from the responsible product/vendor owner.

## Creator segments

Maintain a living creator pool including:

- female lifestyle / fashion creators;
- moms / family lifestyle creators;
- RV / camping / vanlife / overland creators;
- outdoor lifestyle creators;
- DIY / home / trades creators where product fit is natural;
- travel / road-trip / gear creators;
- micro-creators with high trust and engagement;
- larger creators with proven conversion potential;
- creator candidates aligned to viral seasonal products.

Follower count is not the sole selection metric. Fit, consistency, audience match, engagement, showcase behavior, content completion and conversion matter.

## Commission / sample control

Commission is SKU-specific, not universal.

Historical 20% commission may be used as a working reference only when approved product economics support it.

Before increasing commission or approving a sample:

**RETAIL PRICE - PROVIDER/SOURCE COST - SHIPPING SUBSIDY - CHANNEL FEES - COMMISSION - PROMO - SAMPLE ALLOCATION - SELLER-FUNDED AFTERSALE COST = EXPECTED CONTRIBUTION.**

Do not knowingly scale a negative-contribution creator offer unless Casey explicitly approves the difference as acquisition spend.

Refundable samples are preferred when they protect working capital and remain practical for the creator/product.

## Fine-clock operating cadence

This program is designed to run continuously with explicit queues rather than ad hoc bursts.

### Queue A — Product readiness
- current creator-ready apparel products;
- current creator-ready outdoor/camping products;
- safe commission ceilings;
- active inventory/fulfillment state;
- viral/trend candidates;
- HOLD items needing economics/source correction.

### Queue B — Creator acquisition
- new creators to scout;
- qualified creators ready for invite;
- invite batches due;
- segment/product matches.

### Queue C — Warm creator activation
- accepted creators;
- showcase additions;
- sample requests;
- sample delivery/post due dates;
- accepted creators not yet posting.

### Queue D — Performance
- posts live;
- clicks;
- orders;
- GMV;
- commission;
- realized contribution;
- top converting creator/product pairs;
- weak pairings for pause/retest.

### Queue E — Feedback / replenishment
- creators requesting different items;
- viral product gaps;
- outdoor product demand signals;
- apparel style/fit/color demand;
- commission pressure;
- provider/fulfillment failures;
- new products required from Apparel Vendor Ops or other product/vendor owners.

Normal loop:

**REFRESH PRODUCT QUEUE → REFRESH CREATOR QUEUE → INVITE → ACTIVATE WARM CREATORS → FOLLOW UP CONTENT → MEASURE SALES → RETURN DEMAND SIGNALS → REFILL PRODUCT QUEUE → REPEAT.**

## Success condition

This program is healthy when:

- creator recruitment is continuous rather than episodic;
- warm creators receive systematic follow-up;
- current eligible products are actively matched to creators;
- apparel creator coverage, especially female/lifestyle, keeps growing;
- camping/outdoor creators and products become a second active growth lane as eligibility allows;
- invites turn into showcase adds, posts and orders;
- converting creators receive deliberate follow-up and better product opportunities;
- affiliate economics remain contribution-safe;
- realized profit, not GMV alone, decides what scales.
