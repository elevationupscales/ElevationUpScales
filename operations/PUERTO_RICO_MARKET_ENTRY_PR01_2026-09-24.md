## Lithium shipment data requirement

For every candidate SKU, collect and reconcile:

- manufacturer;
- exact model/SKU;
- voltage / amp-hours;
- Wh rating;
- applicable UN classification;
- matching SDS/MSDS;
- matching UN38.3 test summary;
- gross packaged weight;
- carton dimensions;
- pallet dimensions/weight when applicable;
- package count;
- terminal / short-circuit protection description;
- packaging / securement description;
- supplier origin;
- new / undamaged / non-recalled status;
- carrier-required marking / labeling;
- offeror / dangerous-goods signatory responsibility.

PHMSA references:
https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/lithiumbatteries
https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/training/hazmat/new-un-requirement-test-summaries

## Puerto Rico tax / release gate

Official starting references:

https://hacienda.pr.gov/node/5341
https://hacienda.pr.gov/ivu/radicacion-de-declaracion-de-importacion-para-no-comerciantes

Open questions:

- Elevation merchant-registration / remote-sales obligations;
- applicable IVU collection and filing responsibility;
- marketplace versus direct-sale responsibility;
- exact-model solar-equipment exemption eligibility;
- freight tax treatment;
- municipal obligations if applicable;
- who files the import declaration and obtains release;
- what customer responsibility, if any, exists at destination.

Do not advertise a tax exemption or fixed tax treatment until transaction-specific treatment is verified.

Corporate registration is a separate question from merchant/tax registration.

Puerto Rico Department of State references:
https://www.estado.pr.gov/corporaciones
https://www.estado.pr.gov/formularios-para-corporaciones

Obtain a Puerto Rico-qualified determination before selecting an operating structure that depends on local stock, staff or installation activity.

## EEI / filing gate

Reference:
https://www.trade.gov/electronic-export-information-eei

Before shipment:

- determine Schedule B classification;
- determine whether EEI filing is required for the actual transaction;
- identify USPPI;
- identify authorized filing agent;
- obtain ITN or valid exemption citation before carrier cutoff when required;
- have the filer confirm aggregation, deadlines and transaction-specific exceptions.

Do not split shipments to evade filing.
