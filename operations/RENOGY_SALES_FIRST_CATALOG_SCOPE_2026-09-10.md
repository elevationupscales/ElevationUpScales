## Owner direction

Elevation did not complete Renogy dealer onboarding, warranty/backorder mapping and catalog-source work merely to remain in research mode.

The Renogy project now has a sales-led operating objective:

**VERIFIED PRODUCT → GOOD LISTING → CUSTOMER TRUST → LIVE DIRECT-SITE SALE → CLEAN FULFILLMENT → REPEATABLE CATALOG EXPANSION**

Protected dealer facts still control activation, but they do not stop catalog construction, merchandising research, listing copy, collection planning, cross-sell design or the next exact-SKU mapping batch.

## Sales-first rules

1. Build the strongest customer-facing products first rather than waiting for the full Renogy catalog.
2. Use Renogy where it complements Elevation's SOK-first battery strategy: solar generation, charging, inverter/charger, monitoring, controls and balance-of-system products.
3. Favor products with clear customer intent, recognizable use cases, strong official merchandising support, useful warranties and cross-sell potential.
4. Build complete, trustworthy listings: exact model/SKU, accurate specifications, approved media, clear use case, compatibility notes, fulfillment state, current price/MAP control and exact warranty language where verified.
5. Use customer-friendly collections and system relationships rather than dumping raw supplier catalog rows onto the store.
6. A missing protected fact gates only PUBLICATION / CHECKOUT for the affected SKU. It does not gate drafting, copy, SEO/category work, comparison structure, cross-sell planning or research on the next SKU.
7. Continue catalog integration while other exact SKUs wait on portal/MAP/media confirmation.
8. Do not claim marketplace authorization. Renogy remains direct-site only under current supplier terms.

## Products not to lead with

Do not lead the first launch with:

- generic Renogy lithium batteries that simply duplicate the primary SOK relationship without a defined gap;
- `RIV4835CSH1S` while the warranty-source conflict remains unresolved;
- products with unresolved exact SKU identity;
- products whose MAP/price control or direct-site sellability cannot be verified;
- large quantities of low-context accessories before core feature products are live.

## Customer-trust listing standard

Every customer-ready Renogy listing should answer, without making the buyer hunt:

1. What is it?
2. Who is it for?
3. What problem does it solve?
4. What systems/batteries is it compatible with?
5. What exact model/SKU am I buying?
6. Is it in stock, preorder, backorder or unavailable?
7. What does Elevation know about fulfillment timing?
8. What exact warranty applies, if verified?
9. What comes in the box?
10. What should I pair it with?
11. Why buy it from Elevation?

Use approved manufacturer media/specs and Elevation-written merchandising copy. Do not simply copy raw supplier descriptions as the entire listing.

## Elevation trust layer

Renogy listings should consistently reinforce:

- **Authorized Renogy Dealer Partner** status where supplier terms allow that factual representation;
- direct Lower-48 dropship fulfillment under the verified dealer program;
- exact-SKU availability/backorder language rather than fake inventory;
- manufacturer-backed warranty/RMA routed through Renogy with Elevation helping document and support the customer;
- clear compatibility and installation-context guidance;
- Elevation's off-grid/RV/system knowledge and ability to combine Renogy charging/solar equipment with the broader Elevation catalog.

Do not claim faster shipping, broader warranty authority, special inventory or marketplace availability without evidence.

Once products go live, rank continued Renogy work using actual Elevation evidence:

- product views;
- add-to-cart rate;
- checkout starts;
- completed orders;

- returns/warranty friction;
- cross-sell attachment;

Products that generate interest/sales should move up the catalog queue. Products that create support burden without contribution should move down unless strategically necessary.
