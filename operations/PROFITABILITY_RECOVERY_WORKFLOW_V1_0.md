## 1. Control

This workflow does **not** create a new Project, manager, hierarchy, duplicate Workboard or second accounting system.

It is a cross-Project profitability execution workflow used by existing Hybrid Management, Company Operations, vendor Projects, channel workers and shared commerce workers.

Existing Project ownership remains intact.

The company operating sequence becomes:

**STOP CASH LEAKS → RELEASE CASH → SELL ONLY POSITIVE-CONTRIBUTION OFFERS → FULFILL CLEANLY → RECORD REALIZED PROFIT → REPEAT WINNERS → PAY DOWN THE RED BALANCE → BUILD RESERVE.**

This workflow narrows sequencing toward cash-positive operation; it does not erase valid OPEN / WAITING / HOLD work already preserved by the Master S.O.P. and Workboard.

## 2. Definition of profit for operations

Do not manage the company from gross sales alone.

For each order/SKU/channel, use:

`EXPECTED CONTRIBUTION = CUSTOMER REVENUE + BUYER-PAID SHIPPING - PRODUCT/SUPPLIER COST - SUPPLIER SHIPPING/FREIGHT - CHANNEL/PAYMENT FEES - AD/PROMOTION COST - ELEVATION-FUNDED DISCOUNTS/CREDITS - VARIABLE PACKING/FULFILLMENT COST - OTHER KNOWN VARIABLE ORDER COSTS`

After the order is complete, replace estimates with actuals:

`REALIZED CONTRIBUTION = ACTUAL CUSTOMER CASH COLLECTED - ACTUAL VARIABLE CASH COSTS ATTRIBUTABLE TO THAT ORDER`

Taxes collected for taxing authorities are not profit.

Unknown costs must remain `UNKNOWN / VERIFY`; do not turn missing information into an assumed margin.

## 3. Definition of "out of the red"

The exact cumulative red balance must come from actual financial records; it must not be guessed from operational receipts.

For management purposes, recovery proceeds through four gates:

1. **STOP DEEPENING THE RED** — no preventable negative-contribution sales or unnecessary new recurring spend.
2. **OPERATING CASH POSITIVE** — current sales activity produces positive realized contribution and current essential obligations are supportable from operating cash.
3. **ROLLING PROFIT POSITIVE** — 7-day and then 30-day realized operating contribution remain positive after current variable costs and ordinary operating expenses.
4. **RED BALANCE = $0** — cumulative owner/company operating deficit has been recovered from realized company cash generation.

After Gate 4, move excess operating profit into reserve before broad expansion.

Until an exact financial baseline is connected/reconciled, the red-balance amount remains **UNKNOWN / FINANCIAL BASELINE REQUIRED** rather than an invented number.

## 4. P0 decision rule

Every executable task is screened in this order:

### A. Prevent loss
Does this action stop a known negative-contribution order, duplicate expense, charge, fee, refund leakage, stale subscription, avoidable freight exposure or unsupported SKU?

If YES, it receives immediate priority inside its lane.

### B. Release cash
Does this action release held payouts, close fulfillment so funds can settle, collect a valid receivable, resolve a payment hold, clear a customer obligation, obtain supplier tracking or remove a preventable payout blocker?

If YES, it outranks catalog expansion and low-probability research.

### C. Produce profitable revenue
Does this action directly increase the probability of selling an already verified positive-contribution offer through a working checkout path?

If YES, it is P0 revenue execution.

### D. Improve profit per order
Does this action reduce current source cost, shipping/freight, channel fee exposure, refund rate, handling time or fulfillment failure without lowering revenue more than the savings?

If YES, execute within established authority.

### E. Everything else
Brand enrichment, catalog size, speculative integrations, cosmetic improvement, broad prospecting and non-urgent research stay behind executable stop-loss, cash-release and positive-contribution sales work.

## 5. SKU / offer gate

No product is actively promoted merely because it is listed.

The standard gate is:

**EXACT SKU → AUTHORIZED SOURCE → CURRENT SELLABILITY / INVENTORY OR AUTHORIZED DELAYED-ORDER PATH → CURRENT PRICE / MAP → DESTINATION ELIGIBILITY → LANDED VARIABLE COST → EXPECTED CONTRIBUTION → WORKING CHECKOUT → FULFILLMENT RELIABILITY → PROMOTE.**

A missing fact holds only that exact SKU.

### SELL
Use when all material facts are verified and expected contribution is positive.

### VERIFY
Keep available for reconciliation where safe, but do not spend traffic/advertising effort until the missing material economic fact is resolved.

### STOP / REBUILD
Do not accept another order under the current configuration when expected contribution is negative, source fulfillment is unreliable or a material compliance/channel condition fails.

### SCALE
Scale only after actual order evidence proves positive realized contribution and clean fulfillment.

## 7. Capital protection rules

During profitability recovery:

1. **No catalog-for-catalog's-sake spending.**
2. **No paid advertising by default.** An exact SKU may use paid promotion only after contribution modeling includes the ad cost and management has actual conversion evidence supporting the spend.
3. **No new recurring software/app/subscription expense** unless the expense is required for a working revenue/compliance function or has a clear near-term savings/revenue case and passes the applicable owner gate.
4. **No speculative inventory purchase by default.** Prefer dropship, customer-paid preorder/backorder, supplier-held stock, direct supplier fulfillment or customer-funded freight where authorized.
5. **No unverified discounts.** Preserve MAP and expected contribution.
6. **No new supplier commitment merely to create product breadth.** Existing greenlit suppliers must first produce executable profit lanes.
7. **No duplicated product/listing/project systems.** Simplification is itself a profitability control.
8. Customer safety, legal, security, fraud, chargeback and material account-risk controls remain protected and can interrupt normal profitability sequencing.

## 8. Profitability ranking

When multiple clean opportunities exist, prioritize the one with the strongest combination of:

1. expected contribution dollars per order;
2. probability of sale / verified demand signal;
3. fulfillment confidence;
4. low cash required before customer payment;
5. low refund/warranty/exception risk;
6. fast cash-conversion cycle;
7. repeatability.

Do not maximize percentage margin while ignoring contribution dollars and realistic demand.

Do not maximize gross revenue while ignoring cash required and variable cost.

The preferred business model is:

**CUSTOMER PAYS FIRST OR NEAR-FIRST → SUPPLIER FULFILLS → ELEVATION RETAINS POSITIVE CONTRIBUTION → CASH RETURNS QUICKLY → REPEAT.**

## 12. Recovery stages

### Stage 0 — STOP THE BLEEDING

Close known negative-contribution configurations, prevent duplicate/avoidable spend and service current customer obligations so losses do not compound.

Exit when no known avoidable negative-contribution configuration remains knowingly saleable without an approved exception.

### Stage 1 — CASH RELEASE

Release held marketplace funds, complete clean fulfillment, collect valid revenue and prevent unnecessary refunds/chargebacks.

Exit when current cash locks/holds are either cleared or isolated as true external waits.

### Stage 2 — PROFITABLE SALES ENGINE

Drive owned/free traffic to a small number of verified direct-site products and retain only profitable marketplace offers.

Exit when repeatable paid orders produce positive realized contribution with clean fulfillment.

### Stage 3 — OPERATING CASH POSITIVE

Use realized profit to support current essential operating expenses rather than relying on additional owner cash for ordinary operating activity.

Exit when trailing operating performance demonstrates sustainable positive cash generation.

### Stage 4 — ERASE THE RED BALANCE

Direct excess operating profit toward the verified cumulative operating deficit until it reaches zero.

Do not declare this stage complete from sales volume. Use actual financial records.

### Stage 5 — RESERVE THEN SCALE

After the red balance reaches zero, accumulate a basic operating reserve before materially increasing paid acquisition, speculative stock or fixed overhead.

Scale only the channels/SKUs with proven realized contribution and reliable fulfillment.
