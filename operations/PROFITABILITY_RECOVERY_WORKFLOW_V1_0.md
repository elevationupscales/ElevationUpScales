## 2. Definition of profit for operations

Do not manage the company from gross sales alone.

For each order/SKU/channel, use:

`EXPECTED CONTRIBUTION = CUSTOMER REVENUE + BUYER-PAID SHIPPING - PRODUCT/SUPPLIER COST - SUPPLIER SHIPPING/FREIGHT - CHANNEL/PAYMENT FEES - AD/PROMOTION COST - ELEVATION-FUNDED DISCOUNTS/CREDITS - VARIABLE PACKING/FULFILLMENT COST - OTHER KNOWN VARIABLE ORDER COSTS`

After the order is complete, replace estimates with actuals:

`REALIZED CONTRIBUTION = ACTUAL CUSTOMER CASH COLLECTED - ACTUAL VARIABLE CASH COSTS ATTRIBUTABLE TO THAT ORDER`

Taxes collected for taxing authorities are not profit.

Unknown costs must remain `UNKNOWN / VERIFY`; do not turn missing information into an assumed margin.

During the current owner paid-ad lock, ad/promotion cost should normally be zero because prohibited prepaid acquisition is not executed. Performance-based affiliate commission and other permitted variable acquisition costs still belong in contribution math.

## 3. Definition of "out of the red"

The exact cumulative red balance must come from actual financial records; it must not be guessed from operational receipts.

For management purposes, recovery proceeds through four gates:

1. **STOP DEEPENING THE RED** — no preventable negative-contribution sales or unnecessary new recurring spend.
2. **OPERATING CASH POSITIVE** — current sales activity produces positive realized contribution and current essential obligations are supportable from operating cash.
3. **ROLLING PROFIT POSITIVE** — 7-day and then 30-day realized operating contribution remain positive after current variable costs and ordinary operating expenses.
4. **RED BALANCE = $0** — cumulative owner/company operating deficit has been recovered from realized company cash generation.

After Gate 4, move excess operating profit into reserve before broad expansion.

Until an exact financial baseline is connected/reconciled, the red-balance amount remains **UNKNOWN / FINANCIAL BASELINE REQUIRED** rather than an invented number.

Paid acquisition does not automatically reopen at Gate 4; Casey must explicitly reopen it.

## 4. P0 decision rule

Every executable task is screened in this order:

### A. Prevent loss
Does this action stop a known negative-contribution order, duplicate expense, charge, fee, refund leakage, stale subscription, avoidable freight exposure or unsupported SKU?

If YES, it receives immediate priority inside its lane.

### B. Release cash
Does this action release held payouts, close fulfillment so funds can settle, collect a valid receivable, resolve a payment hold, clear a customer obligation, obtain supplier tracking or remove a preventable payout blocker?

If YES, it outranks catalog expansion and low-probability research.

### C. Produce profitable revenue
Does this action directly increase the probability of selling an already verified positive-contribution offer through a working checkout path using an allowed traffic method?

If YES, it is P0 revenue execution.

### D. Improve profit per order
Does this action reduce current source cost, shipping/freight, channel fee exposure, refund rate, handling time or fulfillment failure without lowering revenue more than the savings?

If YES, execute within established authority.

### E. Everything else
Brand enrichment, catalog size, speculative integrations, cosmetic improvement, broad prospecting and non-urgent research stay behind executable stop-loss, cash-release and positive-contribution sales work.

## 5. SKU / offer gate

No product is actively promoted merely because it is listed.

**EXACT SKU → AUTHORIZED SOURCE → CURRENT SELLABILITY / INVENTORY OR AUTHORIZED DELAYED-ORDER PATH → CURRENT PRICE / MAP → DESTINATION ELIGIBILITY → LANDED VARIABLE COST → EXPECTED CONTRIBUTION → WORKING CHECKOUT → FULFILLMENT RELIABILITY → PROMOTE.**

A missing fact holds only that exact SKU.

### SELL
Use when all material facts are verified and expected contribution is positive.

During the owner paid-ad lock, SELL/PROMOTE means organic/owned or another allowed no-prohibited-upfront-spend channel only.

### VERIFY
Keep available for reconciliation where safe, but do not spend concentrated traffic effort until the missing material economic fact is resolved.

An owning lane may preserve an ACTIVE listing while classifying traffic `HOLD — ECONOMICS UNKNOWN`; ACTIVE storefront state alone does not equal promotion approval.

### STOP / REBUILD
Do not accept another order under the current configuration when expected contribution is negative, source fulfillment is unreliable or a material compliance/channel condition fails.

### SCALE
Scale only after actual order evidence proves positive realized contribution and clean fulfillment, and only through acquisition methods allowed by the current owner cash controls.

Direct excess operating profit toward the verified cumulative operating deficit until it reaches zero.

Do not declare this stage complete from sales volume. Use actual financial records.

### Stage 5 — RESERVE THEN SCALE

After the red balance reaches zero, accumulate a basic operating reserve before materially increasing speculative stock or fixed overhead.
