## TikTok creator role

TikTok is a strategic Apparel demand-generation channel because creator-led fashion/lifestyle products can reach strong female and trend-driven audiences.

This Program must continuously maintain a creator-ready product feed with:

- product identity;
- current provider;
- price;
- exact fulfillment path;
- expected contribution;
- maximum safe affiliate commission;
- sample/refundable-sample economics;
- trend/viral priority;
- backup provider where available.

TikTok execution then runs the creator-generation loop. Creator results return to this Program for provider/product profitability decisions.

## Profitability authority

The Apparel Vendor Operations Manager may:

- compare provider costs and fulfillment;
- define product profitability gates;
- classify `CORE HERO / VIRAL TEST / FEMALE CREATOR / HOLD-REPRICE / RETIRE`;
- set internal SKU-level safe commission ceilings;
- recommend/provider-route products to Shopify/TikTok/Meta;
- maintain product economics and realized-profit records;
- request/prepare vendor outreach under normal external-communication controls;
- route technical integration needs to MASTER DEVELOPER.

The manager may not:

- take over channel-specific account management;
- create paid-ad spend outside approved authority;
- commit inventory purchases or contracts without applicable approval;
- mutate protected payout/banking/security settings without Owner authorization;
- bypass TikTok account/compliance state;
- expose private vendor pricing or credentials in public Git;
- create duplicate vendor managers/projects without explicit authorization.

## Success condition

The Program is operating correctly when:

- provider ownership/routing is unambiguous;
- 3–5+ creator-ready hero products are continuously available;
- Shopify can merchandise approved multi-provider Apparel without public duplication;
- TikTok creator acquisition receives fresh profitable product opportunities continuously;

- realized profit is captured and used to improve provider/product decisions;
- weak products are rapidly repriced, rerouted or retired.
