## Interpretation control

The current public evidence shows a meaningful spread in displayed hardware prices for comparable 48V / ~5.12kWh rack batteries, but it does **not** establish Elevation's competitive landed price.

Do not compare displayed sticker prices as if they include the same:

- tax treatment;
- Puerto Rico freight;
- terminal release;
- delivery;
- cables/rack accessories;
- warranty logistics;
- physical stock;
- customer support;
- installation;
- financing/payment costs.

The exact SOK SK48V100N remains the preferred rack-battery candidate for PR-02 because Elevation already has an authorized SOK relationship and can qualify the exact supplier/warranty/freight path directly.
