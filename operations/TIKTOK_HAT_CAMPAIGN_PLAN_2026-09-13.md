## 3. Campaign objective

Primary:

**Produce repeatable creator-driven TikTok Shop sales at positive realized contribution using one hero hat before expanding the Apparel affiliate catalog.**

Secondary:

- identify which creator audience converts best;
- identify which hat color converts best;
- generate reusable organic UGC;
- establish a repeatable creator outreach + sample workflow;

Not a goal:

- maximizing vanity views;
- mass creator seeding;
- joining every TikTok campaign;
- buying paid traffic;
- pushing the whole 29-product Fourthwall catalog.

## 4. Market positioning

Current TikTok Shop hat discovery continues to feature baseball caps, snapbacks, adjustable caps and outdoor/casual styles. Fall 2026 fashion coverage also favors outdoors influence and personality-driven accessories.

Elevation should not compete as a commodity $9 hat.

Position the Mountain Patch cap as:

**A clean outdoor/workwear cap with a real company identity behind it — wearable at the jobsite, campsite, RV, trail, shop, or around town.**

### Core customer promise

**It looks like a hat you chose, not promotional merch you were handed.**

## 6. Creator acquisition structure

Do not optimize for follower count alone.

### Cohort 1 — DISCOVERY

Target roughly **15–25 creators** for the first controlled outreach wave once margin is validated.

Preferred traits:

- consistent recent shoppable posts;
- actual Shop sales or unit movement;
- reliable posting history;
- audience overlap with one of the three lanes;
- useful on-camera product demonstration;
- no obvious dependence on massive discounts to convert.

Typical size can skew micro/small because cost and authenticity matter more than celebrity reach.

### Cohort 2 — PROVEN CONVERTERS

Creators from Cohort 1 who produce attributed orders or unusually strong product clicks/content become Target Collaboration candidates.

Possible rewards after economics allow:

- higher commission tier;
- selective free sample;
- first access to next color/hat;
- direct re-invite on future Apparel launches.

### Cohort 3 — PARTNERS

Only creators with repeated profitable sales move here.

They become candidates for:

- highest safe Target commission;
- repeat product drops;
- multi-hat creator bundle/content;
- ongoing Elevation Gear relationship.

## 7. Commission architecture

**NO RATE IS APPROVED UNTIL EXACT FOURTHWALL BASE COST + CURRENT TIKTOK RATE + CHANNEL LEAKAGE ARE VERIFIED.**

Planning ladder only:

- Open discovery: target **up to 10%** only if safe contribution allows;
- Proven Target: **up to 15%** only if safe contribution allows;
- Strategic/Partner: **up to 20%** only if safe contribution allows.

The live TikTok mission offering visibility for **2%+ commission** is not a reason to launch at 2% or any other number before the true cost is known.

Use tiered commissions later if the account economics support them; stronger creators should earn higher rates through sales rather than every creator receiving the top rate from day one.

## 8. Sample strategy

Current live account state: **23 products have samples disabled, 0 sample requests.**

That is acceptable until the hero clears margin.

### Phase-one sample model

Preferred default after profitability clears:

**REFUNDABLE SAMPLE → 2 QUALIFYING SALES → REFUND**

Adjust only from economics:

- 1 sale = strategic low-friction creator only;
- 2 sales = normal launch starting point;
- 3 sales = margin-constrained / higher acquisition cost.

### Free samples

Do not broadly enable.

Reserve for:

- creator already proven to convert;
- unusually high brand/audience fit;
- owner-approved strategic creator;
- controlled content launch requiring a physical sample.

Use TikTok's SKU-level sample controls if needed to limit creator sampling to selected colorways.

## 9. Content system

Every creator does not need the same script. The campaign needs repeatable **angles**.

### Angle 1 — JOBSITE TO WEEKEND

Hook examples:

- `The hat I wear when I don't want to switch from work mode to weekend mode.`
- `A work hat that doesn't look like company swag.`
- `Jobsite at 3. Dinner at 6. Same hat.`

Required shots:

1. immediate face/hat reveal;
2. front patch close-up;
3. side profile;
4. back adjustment;
5. 2–3 seconds wearing it in a real work/shop environment;
6. product-card CTA.

### Angle 2 — OFF-GRID / OUTDOOR

Hook examples:

- `POV: your everyday hat needs to survive camp, town, and the drive home.`
- `The Colorado-style cap that actually fits an RV/off-grid weekend.`
- `Trail hat without the giant outdoor-brand billboard.`

Show:

- camp/RV/trail context;
- Dark Green/Natural or Navy/Natural;
- close-up of mountain patch;
- adjustable closure;
- quick fit check.

### Angle 3 — DOESN'T LOOK LIKE MERCH

Hook examples:

- `This is technically brand merch. It doesn't look like brand merch.`
- `POV: you wanted a logo hat that still looks clean.`
- `I would actually wear this even if nobody paid me to.`

This is the strongest creator-authenticity angle and should be tested aggressively.

### Angle 4 — COLOR PICK

Creator shows two or three colors and asks viewers which they would wear.

Use comments as product research:

- Black vs Dark Green/Natural;
- Black/Natural vs Navy/Natural;
- workwear vs outdoor look.

### Angle 5 — FIT / DETAIL

Short conversion-focused video:

- structured front;
- breathable eyelets;
- adjustable snap;
- underside / sweatband;
- front patch detail;
- one-size positioning.

## 12. 14-day pilot structure

### PRE-LAUNCH — GATE

Must clear:

1. exact Fourthwall product/base cost;
2. current TikTok Open commission;
3. Shop Ads commission if any;
4. Auto-add state;
5. seller-funded shipping/promos;
6. safe commission ceiling.

### DAYS 1–3 — HERO LIVE

Once gate clears:

- Mountain Patch only;
- one safe Open Collaboration rate;
- refundable sampling only if contribution supports it;
- publish Elevation-owned first 2–3 videos;
- begin first creator invite wave.

### DAYS 4–7 — CREATIVE SIGNAL

Measure:

- creator accept rate;
- videos posted;
- product views;
- product clicks;
- add-to-cart where available;
- orders;
- contribution per order;
- top color/content angle.

Do not increase commission merely because views are weak.

### DAYS 8–14 — DOUBLE DOWN

Move only profitable/strong creators to Target Collaboration.

Actions may include:

- higher safe rate for proven creator;
- more creators matching winning audience type;
- repeat winning hook with different creators;
- concentrate on winning color;
- selectively approve/free-sample a creator with demonstrated sales ability.

## 13. Pilot KPIs

### Minimum operating scoreboard

Per creator + product:

- invite sent;
- invite accepted;
- sample type/status;
- content posted;
- views;
- clicks;
- attributed orders;
- GMV;
- commission dollars;
- provider cost;
- TikTok fees;
- promotion/discount cost;
- realized contribution dollars;
- realized contribution percent;
- returns/refunds;
- creator tier;
- next action.

### Campaign gates

**GREEN**

- attributed orders;
- positive contribution after commission;
- creator/content pattern repeats;
- acceptable fulfillment experience.

**YELLOW**

- strong views/clicks but no orders;
- orders but weak contribution;
- one creator works but others do not.

Action: improve offer, page, hook, creator fit or provider economics — not automatic commission increases.

**RED**

- negative contribution;
- high sample leakage;
- refund/return issue;
- persistent low conversion across multiple qualified creators;
- fulfillment failure.

Action: pause, reprice, reroute provider, or replace hero.
