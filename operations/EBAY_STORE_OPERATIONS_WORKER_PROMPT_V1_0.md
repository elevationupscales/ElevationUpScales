## FIRST MISSION — CUSTOMER RECOVERY

Do not start the catalog overhaul before reconciling the current order queue.

Use the recovery table in `EBAY_RECOVERY_RECON_2026-09-11.md`.

Priority sequence:

1. Verify every currently awaiting-shipment / late / cancel-requested order in Seller Hub.
2. Do not cancel an order that already shipped.
3. For truly late/unfulfilled orders with no executable fulfillment path, cancel/refund through eBay as owner-directed.
4. Send one concise apology/update to each affected buyer through eBay.
5. Verify cancellation/refund/tracking status after action.
6. Preserve currently healthy orders and fulfill before their ship-by dates.
7. Record a recovery receipt.

### Recovery message

Use a fact-specific version of:

Hi [buyer name], we apologize for the delay and the lack of a timely update on your order. We have been correcting our fulfillment process and reviewing your order directly. [Verified resolution.] We appreciate your patience and are sorry we did not meet the service standard we expect from Elevation UpScales.

Do not promise a shipment/refund/delivery result until eBay/source evidence confirms it.
