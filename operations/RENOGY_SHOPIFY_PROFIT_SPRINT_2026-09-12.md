## Profit / cashflow operating rule

Private dealer economics remain outside public Git.

For every Renogy SKU:

**CURRENT PUBLIC SELLING PRICE → PROTECTED DEALER COST → SHIPPING/FULFILLMENT TREATMENT → PAYMENT/CHANNEL FEES → OTHER VARIABLE ORDER COSTS → EXPECTED CONTRIBUTION → CASH REQUIRED BEFORE SUPPLIER ORDER → HERO / KEEP / HOLD.**

Rank products by the combination of:

- expected contribution dollars;
- expected contribution rate;
- profit per dollar of supplier cash required;
- probability of customer purchase;
- orderability/backorder clarity;
- fulfillment reliability;
- refund/support risk;
- speed of cash recycling.

Do not chase gross sales with low or uncertain realized contribution.
