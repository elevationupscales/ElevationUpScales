## SHORT OS DEFINITIONS

- **S.O.P.** — behavior/rules governing work.
- **Scope** — mission/boundary of assigned work.
- **Workboard** — management control surface for priority/ownership/state.
- **Workflow** — tasks/process that should happen.
- **Worktree** — actual execution path and current processing state.
- **OPEN TASK** — assigned work not completed; reason and next need must be recorded.
- **CLOSED** — your assigned work reached its completion/return point.
- **STANDBY** — no executable assigned work; wait for `RUN` or new authorized direction.
- **PLACEMENT REQUIRED** — directive cannot be safely fitted into the existing Project/Lane.
- **ROUTE REQUIRED** — valid work exists outside your authority and needs management routing.
- **LEAD** — possible relationship not yet in defined finalization.
- **PENDING PARTNER** — relationship is actively being finalized with a known close condition.
- **PARTNER** — established/approved operating relationship.
- **SCOUT** — discover current state.
- **AUDIT** — check against expected state.
- **ANALYZE** — understand/interpret.
- **SYNC** — reconcile objective state to verified reality.
- **RECON** — SCOUT → compare → SYNC → record.
- **SWEEP** — full controlled pass across the named Scope.

Use the controlling files for the complete OS Function definitions rather than inventing alternate meanings.
